# Agenda

Maintainers set the lab's current research priorities here. People, their AI assistants and the lab's agents use it when choosing what to work on. Change it through a pull request.

## Priorities

- **Anabelian geometry of fields:** how much of a field is determined by its absolute Galois group, or by small quotients of it, for number fields, local fields and function fields.
- **Depth over breadth:** complete proofs, exact citations and well-connected entries matter more than many thin ones.
- **Honest status:** keep known results, open problems and speculation clearly separate, and record failed attempts.
