**Setting.** Let $k$ be a finite extension of $\mathbb{Q}_p$, with valuation ring $\mathcal{O}_k$, $p$-adic valuation $v$ and residue field $F$. Let $X/k$ be a smooth, geometrically connected [hyperbolic curve](hyperbolic-curve.md), $K = k(X)$ its function field, and $\tilde K$ the function field of the universal pro-étale cover of $X$. So $\pi_1(X) = \mathrm{Gal}(\tilde K/K)$, and it is an extension of $G_k$ by $\pi_1(X_{\bar k})$ [@popstix2017, §1.1 and A.6, pp. 1 and 29]. Write $\mathrm{Val}_v(K)$ and $\mathrm{Val}_v(\tilde K)$ for the sets of valuations of $K$ and of $\tilde K$ that prolong $v$. The group $\pi_1(X)$ acts on $\mathrm{Val}_v(\tilde K)$, and the decomposition group $D_{\tilde w|w}$ of $\tilde w$, where $w = \tilde w|_K$, is its stabilizer [@popstix2017, §5.1.2, p. 18].

A $k$-rational point $a$ of $X$ gives the discrete valuation $w_a$ of $K$ at $a$. For a prolongation $\tilde w_a$ to $\tilde K$, the projection $D_{\tilde w_a} \to G_k$ is an isomorphism, and its inverse is a section in the conjugacy class $[s_a]$ attached to $a$ [@popstix2017, §1.4, p. 2]. Pop and Stix formulate a *valuative section conjecture*: for $k$ a number field or finite over $\mathbb{Q}_p$ and $X$ smooth projective of genus at least $2$, every section $s$ of $\pi_1(X) \to G_k$ should satisfy $s(G_k) \subseteq D_{\tilde w}$ for some valuation $w$ of $K$, trivial on $k$, with residue field $k$. They note that this is equivalent to the section conjecture over number fields and to its $p$-adic version over finite extensions of $\mathbb{Q}_p$ [@popstix2017, Conjectures 1–3, pp. 1–2].

**Theorem (Pop–Stix).** For every section $s : G_k \to \pi_1(X)$ there is $\tilde w \in \mathrm{Val}_v(\tilde K)$ with $s(G_k) \subseteq D_{\tilde w|w}$ [@popstix2017, Theorem 26, p. 18].

For $X$ projective of genus at least $2$, this is part (1) of their Main Result [@popstix2017, §1.5.3, p. 3]. The valuation prolongs $v$ instead of being trivial on $k$, which is the gap between the theorem and the conjecture.

**Types of valuations.** Pop and Stix sort the valuations $w \in \mathrm{Val}_v(K)$ by their centres on regular, proper, flat models over $\mathcal{O}_k$ whose reduced special fibres have strict normal crossings [@popstix2017, A.1.1 and A.2, pp. 25–27]:

- *Type 1v:* the discrete valuation of an irreducible component of the special fibre of some model. Its residue field is a function field of one variable over $F$.
- *Type 2h:* $w = v_{\kappa(a)} \circ w_a$ for a closed point $a$ of $X$, where $v_{\kappa(a)}$ is the $p$-adic valuation of the residue field $\kappa(a)$. The map $w_a \mapsto v_{\kappa(a)} \circ w_a$ embeds the valuations of $K$ trivial on $k$ into $\mathrm{Val}_v(K)$ [@popstix2017, §1.5.2, p. 3].
- *Type 2v:* $w = v_y \circ \alpha$ for a valuation $\alpha$ of type 1v and a closed point $y$ of its component.
- *Type 2u:* the remaining valuations, whose centres move away from every valuation of type 1. They split into the types $2\mathrm{u}_{\mathrm{smooth}}$, $2\mathrm{u}_{\mathrm{node}}$ and $2\mathrm{u}_{\mathrm{alt}}$ [@popstix2017, A.2.3–A.4.3, pp. 26–28].

In rigid-analytic terms, a valuation of type 2h corresponds to discs shrinking to a point of $X^{\mathrm{rig}}$, and one of type $2\mathrm{u}_{\mathrm{smooth}}$ to discs shrinking to a point of $X(\widehat{\bar k}) \setminus X(\bar k)$, where $\widehat{\bar k}$ is the completion of $\bar k$ [@popstix2017, A.3–A.4.4, pp. 27–28]. The residue fields are listed in their table [@popstix2017, A.5, p. 28].

**Consequences.** Let $s$ be a section and $\tilde w$ as in the theorem, with $w = \tilde w|_K$.

1. *Type 2h is geometric.* If $w$ has type 2h and refines $w_a$, then $a \in X(k)$ and $s = s_a$. So the $p$-adic section conjecture reduces to eliminating, in the theorem, the valuations of types other than 2h [@popstix2017, §6.1, p. 21]. Pop and Stix describe this as reducing the conjecture to a completely local problem: to show that $D_{\tilde w} \to G_k$ does not split when $w$ does not originate from a $k$-rational point [@popstix2017, §1.5.3, pp. 3–4].
2. *Residue field.* $F$ is relatively algebraically closed in $\kappa(w)$. Hence $w$ is not of type $2\mathrm{u}_{\mathrm{smooth}}$, and if $w$ has type 1v, then $\kappa(w)$ is a regular function field over $F$ [@popstix2017, §6.2, p. 21]. The [reduction obstruction](reduction-obstruction-to-p-adic-sections.md) turns this into a criterion for the nonexistence of sections, with an example over $\mathbb{Q}_2$.
3. *Type 2.* Let $X$ be proper and $w$ of type 2. For every model with reduced special fibre $Y$, the composite $\mathrm{sp} \circ s|_{I_k} : I_k \to \pi_1(Y_{\bar F})$ is trivial, and the induced section of $\pi_1(Y) \to G_F$ is the section of the $F$-rational point given by the centre of $w$ on $Y$ [@popstix2017, §6.3.1, p. 21]. Moreover $\mathrm{Br}(k) \otimes \mathbb{Z}_\ell \to \mathrm{Br}(K^h_w) \otimes \mathbb{Z}_\ell$ is injective for all $\ell \neq p$, where $K^h_w$ is a henselization [@popstix2017, §6.3.2, p. 22].
4. *Uniqueness.* Let $X' \to X$ be a finite étale cover with a Galois-equivariant model $\mathcal{X}'$ and stable model $\mathcal{X}'_{\mathrm{stable}}$, and put $\Sigma = s(G_k)$. The centres on the special fibre of $\mathcal{X}'_{\mathrm{stable}}$ of the valuations in $\mathrm{Val}_v(\tilde K)$ fixed by $\Sigma$ are either a unique closed $F$-rational point, or the generic point of a unique component together with a closed $F$-rational point on it, or the generic point of a unique component [@popstix2017, Theorem 40, p. 24]. Call a valuation of type 1v *visible* if its component is covered by a component of positive genus in the reduction of a model of some finite étale cover [@popstix2017, Definition 13, p. 12]. If $X$ has a cofinal system of finite étale covers with models whose special fibre components are all visible, then either $\tilde w$ is unique, or there is a unique $\tilde\alpha$ of type 1v with a refinement $\tilde w = \tilde v_y \circ \tilde\alpha$ of type 2v such that $s(G_k) \subseteq D_{\tilde w} \subseteq D_{\tilde\alpha}$ [@popstix2017, Corollary 41, p. 25].

**Method.** The existence proof uses the prime-to-$p$ part of $\pi_1(X_{\bar k})$ [@popstix2017, §1.7, p. 4].

- Let $\tilde X^s$ be the quotient of the universal cover by $s(G_k)$, the largest subcover to which $s$ lifts. For $\ell \neq p$, the map $\mathrm{Br}(k) \otimes \mathbb{Z}_\ell \to \mathrm{Br}(\tilde X^s) \otimes \mathbb{Z}_\ell$ is an isomorphism [@popstix2017, §4.4.1 and Proposition 24, p. 16]. The proof uses that every finite subcover has index a power of $p$, by Stix's [period and index theorem](section-conjecture-period-index.md).
- Pop's local–global principle says that for a function field $M$ of one variable over $k$, the restriction $\mathrm{Br}(M) \to \prod_{w | v} \mathrm{Br}(M^h_w)$ is injective [@popstix2017, Theorem 20, p. 14]. Applied to the function field of $\tilde X^s$ and the class of invariant $1/\ell$, it gives a valuation $w$ such that an $\ell$-Sylow subgroup of $s(G_k)$ lies in $D_{\tilde w}$, and $s$ maps an $\ell$-Sylow subgroup of $I_k$ into the inertia group $I_{\tilde w}$ [@popstix2017, Theorem 25, p. 16].
- The space $\mathrm{Val}_v(\tilde K)$ is a profinite limit of the special fibres of equivariant models of finite Galois covers, with the constructible topology. By compactness it is enough to find a fixed point of $s(G_k)$ on each of these special fibres [@popstix2017, proof of Theorem 26 and Theorem 27, p. 18]. This uses Mochizuki's sturdy covers, the independence of the $\ell$-adic inertia groups of components of positive genus, a lemma on finite group actions, and fixed points of group actions on finite trees [@popstix2017, Lemma 28, Proposition 4, Lemma 31 and Lemmas 32–33, pp. 8 and 18–20].

The uniqueness results use the $p$-part as well, through Tamagawa's resolution of non-singularities [@popstix2017, §1.7, p. 4, and Theorem 36, p. 22].

**What remains open.** As of this source, the $p$-adic section conjecture is open, and so are the following.

- *Visibility.* Pop asked whether all components are visible, and the authors could not resolve this [@popstix2017, Question 14, p. 12]. Tamagawa's work only shows that over each closed point of the stable model, fine enough models have visible non-stable rational components [@popstix2017, Remark 15 (1), p. 12].
- *Invisible components.* Pop and Stix find it conceivable that invisible projective lines contribute sections localized in a $p$-adic disc but not at a $k$-rational point, which would make the $p$-adic section conjecture fail [@popstix2017, §7.3, p. 25].
- *Tempered sections.* Mochizuki showed that a section of the tempered fundamental group fixes a compatible system of vertices or edges of the dual graphs of stable models. The authors could not decide whether every profinite section is conjugate to one with image in the tempered group [@popstix2017, §1.6, p. 4].
- *Berkovich points.* Pop and Stix credit Kedlaya with the interpretation that a section gives at least a $k$-Berkovich point responsible for it, and they leave open which such points contribute sections [@popstix2017, §1.5.3, p. 4].
