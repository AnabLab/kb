**Setting.** Fix a prime $\ell$. Let $\pi^{(\ell)}$ be the pro-$\ell$ completion of $\pi_1(\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{C}) \setminus \{0, 1, \infty\}, x)$, with lower central series $\pi^{(\ell)} = L^1 \supseteq L^2 \supseteq \cdots$, and let $\phi_\ell : G_{\mathbb{Q}} \to \mathrm{Out}\, \pi^{(\ell)}$ be the outer Galois action. Ihara's filtration is
$$I^m_\ell G_{\mathbb{Q}} = \ker\big(G_{\mathbb{Q}} \to \mathrm{Out}(\pi^{(\ell)}/L^{m+1})\big), \qquad m \ge 0.$$
It does not depend on the base point. The cyclotomic character identifies $G_{\mathbb{Q}}/I^1_\ell G_{\mathbb{Q}}$ with $\mathbb{Z}_\ell^\times$, so $I^1_\ell G_{\mathbb{Q}}$ is the absolute Galois group of $\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{\ell^\infty})$. Since $[I^m_\ell G_{\mathbb{Q}}, I^n_\ell G_{\mathbb{Q}}] \subseteq I^{m+n}_\ell G_{\mathbb{Q}}$, the positive part
$$\mathfrak{g}^\ell = \bigoplus_{m \ge 1} I^m_\ell G_{\mathbb{Q}} / I^{m+1}_\ell G_{\mathbb{Q}}$$
of the associated graded group is a graded Lie algebra over $\mathbb{Z}_\ell$, with bracket induced by the group commutator [@hainmatsumoto2003, §1, p. 1]. For odd $\ell$, its degree-$m$ part is isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}_\ell(m)^{\oplus r_m}$ for some $r_m \ge 0$, as a module over $\mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}(\mu_{\ell^\infty})/\mathbb{Q})$ [@sharifi2002, §1, p. 1].

**Soulé elements.** For each odd $m \ge 3$, Ihara constructs an element $\sigma_m$ of degree $m$ in $\mathfrak{g}^\ell$, called a Soulé element [@hainmatsumoto2003, §1, p. 2]. Sharifi takes $\sigma_m$ in $I^m_\ell G_{\mathbb{Q}}$ such that its image under the $m$-th Soulé character $\kappa_m$ generates the image of $I^m_\ell G_{\mathbb{Q}}$ [@sharifi2002, §1, p. 1]. Ihara's work shows that each $\sigma_m$ is nonzero in $H_1(\mathfrak{g}^\ell \otimes \mathbb{Q}_\ell)$ [@hainmatsumoto2003, Remark 8.6].

**Theorem.** For every prime $\ell$, the Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}^\ell \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}_\ell} \mathbb{Q}_\ell$ is freely generated by $\sigma_3, \sigma_5, \sigma_7, \dots$ [@brown2019, §2.1, Conjecture 1, and §2.3, p. 4], [@ishii2026, §1, p. 2].

**History.** Ihara attributes to Deligne the conjecture that $\mathfrak{g}^\ell \otimes \mathbb{Q}_\ell$ is generated by elements $s_3, s_5, s_7, \dots$ with $s_{2n+1}$ of degree $2n+1$. Ihara asked whether his $\sigma_m$ generate it freely, and proved that if $\mathfrak{g}^\ell \otimes \mathbb{Q}_\ell$ is free, then it is generated by the $\sigma_m$ [@hainmatsumoto2003, §1, pp. 1–2]. Brown calls the freeness statement the Deligne–Ihara conjecture [@brown2019, §2.1, p. 2]. The papers of Ihara cited for this have not been opened for this entry.

**Proof.**

- *Generation (Hain–Matsumoto).* Hain and Matsumoto prove Deligne's generation conjecture [@hainmatsumoto2003, §8.4]. With Ihara's nonvanishing they get $H_1(\mathfrak{g}^\ell \otimes \mathbb{Q}_\ell) \cong \bigoplus_{m \ge 1} \mathbb{Q}_\ell(2m+1)$, with the summand $\mathbb{Q}_\ell(2m+1)$ spanned by the image of $\sigma_{2m+1}$ [@hainmatsumoto2003, Remark 8.6]. In a positively graded Lie algebra, the image of any section of the projection to $H_1$ generates [@hainmatsumoto2003, Proposition 2.1]. Applied to the section sending the image of $\sigma_{2m+1}$ to $\sigma_{2m+1}$, this shows that the Soulé elements generate.
- *How generation is proved.* The outer action factors through the Galois group $G_\ell$ of the maximal extension of $\mathbb{Q}$ unramified outside $\ell$, by Ihara. Hain and Matsumoto form the weighted completion of $G_\ell$ relative to the cyclotomic character. Its prounipotent radical has a free Lie algebra, and its weight-graded quotient is free on one generator of weight $-2(2n+1)$ for each $n \ge 0$ [@hainmatsumoto2003, Theorem 7.3]. Exactness of the weight-graded functor, called strictness and familiar from Deligne's mixed Hodge theory, identifies $\mathfrak{g}^\ell \otimes \mathbb{Q}_\ell$ in each positive degree $m$ with the weight $-2m$ part of the image of this Lie algebra in the outer derivations of the $\ell$-adic unipotent completion of $\pi_1$ [@hainmatsumoto2003, §1, p. 3, and Theorem 8.4]. The generator of weight $-2$ maps to zero [@hainmatsumoto2003, Lemma 8.5], and this explains why the generators of $\mathfrak{g}^\ell$ start in degree $3$. Hain and Matsumoto write that their techniques do not shed light on freeness [@hainmatsumoto2003, §1, p. 2].
- *Freeness (Brown).* Brown proves that the motivic fundamental group of $\mathbb{P}^1 \setminus \{0, 1, \infty\}$ generates the category of mixed Tate motives over $\mathbb{Z}$, which he notes implies the Deligne–Ihara conjecture [@brown2012, Abstract and §1, p. 1]. He restates this as the faithfulness of the action of the de Rham motivic Galois group of mixed Tate motives over $\mathbb{Z}$ on the de Rham fundamental groupoid, a statement conjectured by Goncharov. That group is an extension of $\mathbb{G}_m$ by a prounipotent group whose graded Lie algebra is free on generators in degrees $-3, -5, \dots$. There is a Zariski-dense homomorphism from $G_{\mathbb{Q}}$ to the $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$-points of the $\ell$-adic realization of the motivic Galois group, and Brown states that faithfulness in the $\ell$-adic realization, at the tangential base point at $0$, proves the conjecture [@brown2019, §2.3, pp. 3–4]. The opened sources do not write out this last deduction in detail.

Ishii summarizes the proof the same way: Hain and Matsumoto proved generation, and Brown later proved the conjecture in full [@ishii2026, §1, p. 2].

**Low degrees.** This is checked here. A free graded Lie algebra on one generator in each odd degree $m \ge 3$ has the following dimensions $d_m$ in degrees $m \le 12$, so by the theorem these are the dimensions of the degree-$m$ parts of $\mathfrak{g}^\ell \otimes \mathbb{Q}_\ell$.

| $m$ | 1, 2, 4, 6 | 3 | 5 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 | 11 | 12 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $d_m$ | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| basis | | $\sigma_3$ | $\sigma_5$ | $\sigma_7$ | $[\sigma_3, \sigma_5]$ | $\sigma_9$ | $[\sigma_3, \sigma_7]$ | $\sigma_{11}$, $[\sigma_3, [\sigma_3, \sigma_5]]$ | $[\sigma_3, \sigma_9]$, $[\sigma_5, \sigma_7]$ |

A bracket of $k$ generators has degree a sum of $k$ odd numbers at least $3$, so it has the parity of $k$ and degree at least $3k$. Degree $12$ therefore allows only $k = 2$, with degrees $\{3, 9\}$ or $\{5, 7\}$, or $k = 4$ with all degrees $3$. Every bracket of four copies of $\sigma_3$ vanishes, since the Lie subalgebra generated by one element is spanned by it. The brackets $[\sigma_3, \sigma_9]$ and $[\sigma_5, \sigma_7]$ are nonzero, as their expansions in the free associative algebra on the generators show, and they are linearly independent because they involve different generators. The other degrees are similar. In degree $11$ the only bracket of three generators that survives is $[\sigma_3, [\sigma_3, \sigma_5]]$, which expands to $\sigma_3^2\sigma_5 - 2\sigma_3\sigma_5\sigma_3 + \sigma_5\sigma_3^2 \ne 0$, since $[\sigma_5, [\sigma_3, \sigma_3]] = 0$ and $[[\sigma_3, \sigma_5], \sigma_3] = -[\sigma_3, [\sigma_3, \sigma_5]]$. As a check, the universal enveloping algebra of this Lie algebra is the free associative algebra on the generators, whose Hilbert series is $1/(1 - t^3 - t^5 - t^7 - \cdots)$, with coefficients $1, 0, 0, 1, 0, 1, 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 4, 5$ in degrees $0$ to $12$. By the Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt theorem these coefficients count multisets of basis elements of total degree $n$. For instance, the $5$ in degree $12$ counts the two brackets above, $\{\sigma_3, \sigma_9\}$, $\{\sigma_5, \sigma_7\}$ and $\{\sigma_3, \sigma_3, \sigma_3, \sigma_3\}$, and the other coefficients agree with the table in the same way.

**Integral and mod-$\ell$ forms.**

- *Integrally.* For odd regular $p$, the map from the free graded $\mathbb{Z}_p$-Lie algebra on generators $s_m$ to $\mathfrak{g}^p$, $s_m \mapsto \sigma_m$, is an isomorphism, given the theorem above [@sharifi2002, Theorem 1]. For an irregular prime $p$ satisfying Greenberg's conjecture, it is not surjective, so $\mathfrak{g}^p$ itself is not generated by the $\sigma_m$ [@sharifi2002, Theorem 3]. McCallum proved Greenberg's conjecture for the irregular primes $p$ for which the $p$-part of the class group of $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_p)$ and the $p$-power torsion of $U/\bar{E}$ both have order $p$, where $U$ is the unit group of $\mathbb{Q}_p(\zeta_p)$ and $\bar{E}$ the closure of the image of $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_p]^\times$ [@sharifi2002, §1, p. 3]. See the [Anderson–Ihara question](anderson-ihara-question.md).
- *Modulo $691$ and modulo depth.* Ihara and Takao found that $[\sigma_3, \sigma_9] - 3[\sigma_5, \sigma_7]$ equals $\tfrac{691}{144}\delta_{12}$ for an element $\delta_{12}$ that Brown describes as nearly zero. Brown writes that this becomes an actual relation modulo the prime $691$ and modulo the depth filtration, and concludes that neither $\mathfrak{g}^\ell \otimes \mathbb{F}_\ell$, for $\ell = 691$, nor the depth-graded form of $\mathfrak{g}^\ell \otimes \mathbb{Q}_\ell$ is freely generated by the $\sigma_m$ [@brown2019, §2.1, p. 2]. Hain and Matsumoto had reported Ihara's congruence in degree $12$ for $\ell = 691$ in his stable derivation algebra, which suggested that $\mathfrak{g}^{691}$ might not be generated by the $\sigma_m$ over $\mathbb{Z}_{691}$ [@hainmatsumoto2003, Remark 8.6].
