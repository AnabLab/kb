**Conjecture (Breuil; solved by Kisin).** Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, choose a uniformizer $\pi$ and compatible roots $\pi_0=\pi$, $\pi_{n+1}^p=\pi_n$, and put $K_\infty=\bigcup_{n\ge0}K(\pi_n)$. Is restriction from crystalline $\mathbb Q_p$-representations of $G_K$ to representations of $G_{K_\infty}$ fully faithful?

Bhatt–Scholze identify this as Breuil's conjecture and state Kisin's affirmative answer [@bhattscholze2023prismatic, §1.2, Theorem 1.4 and preceding sentence, p. 4]. The [full-faithfulness theorem](kummer-tower-full-faithfulness-for-crystalline-representations.md) records the answer and its integral form. This entry retains the original question as a problem.
