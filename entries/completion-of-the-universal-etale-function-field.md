**Setting.** Let $k$ be a finite extension of $\mathbb{Q}_p$, and $X/k$ a smooth, proper, geometrically connected curve of genus at least $2$. Put $K = k(X)$, fix a separable closure $K^{\mathrm{sep}}$ containing $\bar k$, and let $\widetilde K \subseteq K^{\mathrm{sep}}$ be the compositum of the function fields of all connected finite étale covers of $X$, with all their $K$-embeddings. Then $\mathrm{Gal}(\widetilde K/K) = \pi_1(X)$ [@openai2026padicsections, §1.2, p. 3]. This is the field $\tilde K$ of the [Pop–Stix valuative section theorem](valuative-section-theorem-for-p-adic-curves.md). A *rank-one valuation* is one with values in $\mathbb{R}$.

**Theorem.** Let $w$ be a rank-one valuation of $K$ extending the $p$-adic valuation of $k$, and $w^{\mathrm{sep}}$ a prolongation of $w$ to $K^{\mathrm{sep}}$. Let $E$ be the completion of $K^{\mathrm{sep}}$, and $H \subseteq E$ the closure of $\widetilde K$, which is its completion. Then $K^{\mathrm{sep}} \subseteq H$ [@openai2026padicsections, Theorem 1.2, p. 3]. Since $H$ is closed and $K^{\mathrm{sep}}$ is dense in $E$, this says that $H = E$.

**Corollary: decomposition groups.** Normalize $w(p) = 1$ and put $\tilde w = w^{\mathrm{sep}}|_{\widetilde K}$. Restriction is an isomorphism of profinite groups
$$D_{w^{\mathrm{sep}}}(K^{\mathrm{sep}}/K) \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ } D_{\tilde w}(\widetilde K/K)$$
[@openai2026padicsections, Corollary 7.1, p. 16]. It is surjective because $\mathrm{Gal}(K^{\mathrm{sep}}/\widetilde K)$ acts transitively on the prolongations of $\tilde w$. For injectivity, an automorphism that preserves the valuation ring of $w^{\mathrm{sep}}$ preserves $w^{\mathrm{sep}}$ itself, since it fixes $p$. So it extends to an isometry of $E$, and if it is trivial on $\widetilde K$, it fixes $H \supseteq K^{\mathrm{sep}}$.

**Residue fields.** This consequence is checked here. Completion changes neither the residue field nor the value group, and a separably closed valued field has an algebraically closed residue field and a divisible value group. So the residue field of $\tilde w$ is algebraically closed and its value group is divisible. For instance, suppose that $X$ has a smooth proper model over $\mathcal{O}_k$ with special fibre $X_s$, and let $w$ be the valuation of $X_s$. Then every finite extension of $\bar{\mathbb{F}}_p(X_s)$, separable or not, embeds in the residue field of the restriction of $\tilde w$ to the function field of some connected finite étale cover of $X_{\bar k}$.

**Genus at least $2$ is needed.** This is proved here.

- *Genus $0$.* For $X = \mathbb{P}^1_k$, the connected finite étale covers are the $\mathbb{P}^1_{k'}$ with $k'/k$ finite, so $\widetilde K = \bar k(t)$. At the Gauss valuation its residue field is $\bar{\mathbb{F}}_p(t)$, which is not algebraically closed.
- *Genus $1$.* Let $X$ be an elliptic curve with good reduction, with smooth proper model $\mathcal{X}$, and let $w$ be the valuation of $X_s$. A connected finite étale cover of $X_{\bar k}$ is an elliptic curve $X'$ mapping to $X_{\bar k}$ by an isogeny, after choosing origins. Defined over a finite extension $k'$, $X'$ has good reduction, by the Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich criterion, since isogenous curves have isomorphic rational Tate modules. By the Néron mapping property, the isogeny and its dual extend to homomorphisms of the abelian schemes, with composite multiplication by the degree. So on special fibres the map is an isogeny, and $\mathcal{X}' \to \mathcal{X}_{\mathcal{O}_{k'}}$ is quasi-finite and proper, hence finite. As $\mathcal{X}'$ is normal, it is the normalization of $\mathcal{X}$ in $k'(X')$. Its special fibre is irreducible, so $w$ has a unique prolongation to $k'(X')$, and its residue field is the function field of the elliptic curve $\mathcal{X}'_s$. The same holds over every finite extension of $k'$. So the residue field of $\tilde w$ is the union of the function fields of genus-$1$ curves $X'_s$ over $\bar{\mathbb{F}}_p$. A finitely generated subfield of transcendence degree $1$ lies in one of them, and a curve dominated by a curve of genus $1$ has genus at most $1$. But some curves of genus at least $2$ map to $X_s$. For $p \ne 2$, a double cover of $X_s$ branched at exactly two points exists and has genus $2$ by Riemann–Hurwitz. For $p = 2$, an Artin–Schreier cover $y^2 + y = f$, where $f$ has a single pole, of order $3$, has genus $3$. Hence the residue field of $\tilde w$ is not algebraically closed, and the conclusion of the theorem fails.

In the proof below, genus at least $2$ enters through the stable skeleton in step 2 and through resolution of nonsingularities in step 3.

**Outline of the proof.** Write $K_0 = \bar k(X)$, let $\Omega$ be the closure of $\bar k$ in $H$, and let $\xi \in X_\Omega^{\mathrm{an}}$ be the Berkovich point given by $K_0 \hookrightarrow H$. For a finite extension $F/K_0$, the *horizontal ramification indices* are those of the corresponding cover of curves over $X_{\bar k}$ at closed points. Fix an odd prime $\ell \ne p$, and let $L_\ell \subseteq K^{\mathrm{sep}}$ be the compositum of all finite Galois extensions $F/K_0$ whose horizontal indices divide $\ell$. It contains $\widetilde K$ [@openai2026padicsections, §2, p. 5]. The proof shows first that $L_\ell \subseteq H$, and then that $K^{\mathrm{sep}} \subseteq H$.

1. *Patching* [@openai2026padicsections, Lemma 3.1, Proposition 3.2 and Lemma 3.3, pp. 6–7]. Let $C \to X_{\bar k}$ be a finite cover with branch set $B$. Around each $b \in B$, choose a small disk $B_b$ that avoids $\xi$, with a coordinate $t_b$, over which $C$ is a disjoint union of disks with $t_b = z_c^{e_c}$. Let $m_b$ be a common multiple of the $e_c$. Suppose that $Y \to X_\Omega$ is a finite cover by a smooth proper connected curve, and that $t_b$ has an $m_b$-th root on the *entire* inverse image in $Y$ of a collar $A_b \subset B_b$. There, with $a_c$ the $(m_b/e_c)$-th power of that root, $Z^{e_c} - t_b = \prod_{\zeta \in \mu_{e_c}} (Z - \zeta a_c)$ with comaximal factors, so the pullback of $C$ is trivial. It therefore glues, over the exterior $U$ of the collars, with trivial covers over the inner disks. Proper GAGA, from Poineau, algebraizes the result to a finite étale cover $V_\Omega \to Y$ with an analytic map to $C_\Omega^{\mathrm{an}}$ over $U$. Suppose that $V_\Omega \to X_\Omega$ comes from some $V \to X_{\bar k}$, and that $\bar k(V)$ embeds in $H$ over $K_0$. Evaluating the analytic map at the resulting $H$-valued point over $\xi \in U$ gives a $K_0$-embedding $\bar k(C) \hookrightarrow H$.
2. *Roots on stable node annuli* [@openai2026padicsections, Lemma 4.2, pp. 9–10]. Let $C/\Omega$ have genus at least $2$, and let $A$ be a node annulus of its stable model, with coordinate $S$. Suppose first that the edge $e$ of $A$ lies on a simple circuit. At each vertex of the circuit, take the Kummer cover of degree $\ell$ of the residue curve that is totally ramified exactly at the two circuit marks. Take $\ell$ identity copies at the other vertices, one edge of expansion factor $\ell$ over each circuit edge, and $\ell$ edges of factor one over each other edge. This is a tame covering of metrized complexes, and it lifts to a finite étale cover with exactly this skeleton [@aminibakerbrugallerabinoff2015, Theorem 7.7, p. 45, and Definitions 4.25 and 4.31, pp. 26–27]. Over $A$ there is then a single annulus, mapping with degree $\ell$, on which $S$ pulls back to a constant times an $\ell$-th power times a unit close to $1$, so it has an $\ell$-th root. A separating edge becomes nonseparating after a connected unramified cover of degree $\ell$ of each side, glued along $\ell$ bridge edges. Such covers exist because, by stability, each side contains a circuit or a vertex of positive genus.
3. *Collar roots* [@openai2026padicsections, Lemma 4.3, p. 11]. Let $b_1, \dots, b_s \in X(\bar k)$, with small disks $B_i$ and coordinates $t_i$. Blowing up the reductions of the $b_i$ in a semistable model puts node annuli inside the $B_i$. After a finite extension of the base field, resolution of nonsingularities gives a finite étale cover $Y_0$ whose stable model maps to the blown-up model [@mochizukitsujimura2024, Theorem A, p. 4, and Proposition 2.4 (iv), p. 50]. At an irrational level of $|t_i|$ inside a chosen node annulus, every point of $Y_0^{\mathrm{an}}$ above it lies in a node annulus of the stable model of $Y_0$ over that node. At points of type 2, and on the open disks of smooth points, $|t_i|$ takes values in $|\Omega^\times|$. By compactness, the same holds above a whole collar $A_i'$. Step 2 applied to these finitely many annuli gives a finite étale cover $Y \to X_\Omega$ on which each $t_i$ has an $\ell$-th root over the entire inverse image of $A_i'$.
4. *First stage: $L_\ell \subseteq H$* [@openai2026padicsections, Proposition 4.4, pp. 11–12]. Let $F/K_0$ be Galois with horizontal indices dividing $\ell$, and apply step 1 with $m_b = \ell$ and the étale cover $Y$ of step 3. Then $V_\Omega$ is étale over $X_\Omega$, so it descends to a finite étale cover $V \to X_{\bar k}$. This uses the invariance of finite étale covers of proper varieties under extension of algebraically closed fields, from SGA 1 [@openai2026padicsections, §2, p. 5]. So $\bar k(V) \subseteq \widetilde K \subseteq H$, and step 1 embeds $F$ in $H$ over $K_0$. As $F/K_0$ is normal, the image is $F$ itself.
5. *Roots of every order* [@openai2026padicsections, Lemma 5.1 and Proposition 5.2, pp. 12–15]. Let $m \ge 1$, choose an integer $D > v_p(m) + 1/(p-1)$, and $\alpha \in \bar k$ with $a = v_p(\alpha) > 2D$. The curve $Z : y^\ell = T(T-1)/(T-\alpha)$ has genus $\ell - 1$. Its stable model consists of two rational components joined by $\ell$ nodes of thickness $a$, so its Jacobian $J$ has a toric uniformization $\mathbb{T}^{\mathrm{an}} \to J^{\mathrm{an}}$ with a free period lattice $\Lambda$. On the $j$-th node annulus, the character attached to the cycle $e_j - e_{j'}$, composed with a lift of the Abel–Jacobi map, has slope one. So it equals $c\,T(1+h)$ with $|h| < p^{-D}$ on the middle collar $D < v_p(T) < a - D$. In $Z' = Z \times_{J,[m]} J$ this character has an $m$-th root over the whole inverse image of the annulus, because $\Lambda$ is torsion free, and $1 + h$ has one by the $p$-adic logarithm and exponential. So a component $Q$ of $Z'$ is a cover of $\mathbb{P}^1$ with ramification indices $1$ or $\ell$, on which $T$ has an $m$-th root over the entire inverse image of the middle collar. Nothing is assumed about $m$. For the uniformization, and its compatibility with the tropical Abel–Jacobi map, the manuscript cites Raynaud, Bosch–Lütkebohmert and Baker–Rabinoff [@openai2026padicsections, §5, p. 13].
6. *Second stage* [@openai2026padicsections, proof of Theorem 1.2, pp. 15–16]. Let $F/K_0$ be finite Galois with cover $C$, and $m_b$ the least common multiple of the ramification indices over $b$. Pull back the cover of step 5, for $m = m_b$, along $T = t_b/\beta_b$, where $|\beta_b|$ is smaller than the radius of $B_b$, and let $W$ be a compositum of these pullbacks. Its horizontal indices divide $\ell$ [@openai2026padicsections, Lemma 3.4, p. 8], and $t_b$ has an $m_b$-th root over the entire inverse image of a collar in $B_b$. Step 1 with $Y = W_\Omega$ gives a finite étale $V \to W$. So $V \to X_{\bar k}$ has horizontal indices dividing $\ell$, and $\bar k(V)$ embeds in $L_\ell \subseteq H$ by step 4. Step 1 then gives $F \subseteq H$, and every element of $K^{\mathrm{sep}}$ lies in such an $F$.

**What was checked here.** Every step of §§2–6 of the manuscript was rebuilt independently by the lab's research agents, and no gap was found. The checks are recorded in [issue 227](https://github.com/AnabLab/kb/issues/227). They include the following.

- Step 1: the factors are comaximal because $\zeta - \zeta'$ is a nonzero constant, so the argument holds when $p \mid e_c$. Lemma 3.3 keeps the whole $H$-valued point, so the analytic map never needs to descend to $\bar k$.
- Step 2: the conditions of a tame covering of metrized complexes [@aminibakerbrugallerabinoff2015, Definitions 2.19 and 2.21, pp. 9–10]. The covering is harmonic. Its ramification divisor [@aminibakerbrugallerabinoff2015, (2.14.1), p. 9] vanishes, by $2g(D') - 2 = \ell\,(2g(D) - 2) + 2(\ell - 1)$ for the Kummer cover $D' \to D$. All indices are $1$ or $\ell$, the covering graph is connected, and the edge lengths lie in $\mathbb{Q}$. Theorem 7.7 allows loop edges, and with no punctures the lift is étale [@aminibakerbrugallerabinoff2015, Definition 4.31 (1), p. 27]. In the separating case, a side with no circuit and no vertex of positive genus would be a tree with total valence $2N - 1 < 3N$, contradicting stability.
- Step 3: Proposition 2.4 (iv) of Mochizuki–Tsujimura gives a stable model mapping to an arbitrary compactified model after a finite extension, which is exactly what is used. No finiteness of the model map is needed. A unit on an open disk has constant absolute value in $|\Omega^\times|$, which excludes the disks of smooth points at the irrational level.
- Step 5: the normalization of $Z$, whose model has two rational components joined by $\ell$ nodes and is stable because $\ell \ge 3$. The Laurent bounds $|h_n| \le 1$ for $n > 0$ and $|h_{-n}| \le |\alpha|^n$. The logarithm estimate, which holds also for $p = 2$ and for $p \mid m$.
- Step 6: the inverse image of the $T$-collar also contains annuli around the other zeros of $t_b$, but only its part inside $B_b$ is used.

The following inputs are used as cited, not re-proved: resolution of nonsingularities, which is a 2024 preprint; the lifting theorem of Amini, Baker, Brugallé and Rabinoff; the uniformization of totally degenerate Jacobians in Baker–Rabinoff's conventions; Poineau's proper GAGA; and SGA 1.
