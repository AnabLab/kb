---
title: Neukirch–Uchida theorem for p-adic fields
kind: theorem
summary: 'Two finite extensions of $\mathbb{Q}_p$ with isomorphic absolute Galois groups are isomorphic as fields.'
edges:
  - {to: absolute-galois-group, label: uses}
---

Let $K$ and $L$ be finite extensions of $\mathbb{Q}_p$. If $G_K \cong G_L$ as profinite groups, then $K \cong L$ as fields.

This is [@uchida1976, Theorem 2].
