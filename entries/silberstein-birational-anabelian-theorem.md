**Statement.** Let $F_1, F_2$ be fields finitely generated and of transcendence degree at least $2$ over $k_1, k_2$, where $k_1$ is $\bar{\mathbb{Q}}$ or $\bar{\mathbb{F}}_p$ and $k_2$ is algebraically closed. Then the canonical map from isomorphisms of purely inseparable closures, up to Frobenius twists, to outer isomorphisms $G_{F_2} \to G_{F_1}$ is a bijection [@silberstein2013, Theorem 1].

**The new case.** The missing case is transcendence degree $2$ over $\bar{\mathbb{Q}}$ [@silberstein2013, Theorem 2]. The other cases are attributed there to Bogomolov–Tschinkel and Pop [@silberstein2013, §1].

**Method.** Instead of reconstructing the field directly, the proof reconstructs a model on which a given set of divisorial inertia groups are the inertia groups of prime divisors, and interprets intersection theory on that model group-theoretically [@silberstein2013, §1].

**Status of the source.** The source is an arXiv preprint, whose arXiv page lists no journal reference as of November 2026.
