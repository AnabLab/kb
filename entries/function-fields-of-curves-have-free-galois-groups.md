**Statement.** Let $K|k$ be a function field of transcendence degree $1$ over an algebraically closed field. Then the absolute Galois group $G_K$ is profinite free on $|k|$ generators, as shown by Douady, Harbater and Pop. Therefore $\Pi^c_K$ encodes only the cardinality $|k|$ of the base field, hence that of $K$ as well [@pop2018, §1].

**Consequence.** The [Bogomolov–Pop conjecture](bogomolov-pop-conjecture.md) must assume transcendence degree at least $2$. This freeness also obstructs Hom-forms in every dimension. A function field $K|k$ has many surjections $G_K \to G_{k(t)}$, coming from relatively closed subfields $k(t)$, and freeness gives many surjections $G_{k(t)} \to G_K$. So $G_K$ has many surjective endomorphisms that are not geometric, in contrast to Mochizuki's results over sub-$p$-adic fields [@pop2003, Introduction, Remarks].

**Attribution.** The freeness results are attributed through [@pop2018, §1]. The original papers have not been opened for this entry.
