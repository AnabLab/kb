**Statement.** Let $\mathcal{F}$ be the category of function fields $K|k$ with $\mathrm{td}(K|k) > 1$ and $k$ an algebraic closure of a finite field of characteristic different from $\ell$. There is a group-theoretic recipe, invariant under profinite group isomorphisms, that recovers every $K \in \mathcal{F}$ from $G^\ell_K$ up to a purely inseparable extension. In particular, for $K, L \in \mathcal{F}$ there is a canonical bijection between isomorphisms of purely inseparable closures up to Frobenius twists and outer isomorphisms $G^\ell_K \to G^\ell_L$ [@pop2003, Theorem].

**A broader statement.** Szamuely records, as work in preparation by Pop, a statement for fields finitely generated over the algebraic closure of the prime field, with transcendence degree at least $2$ [@szamuely2004, Théorème 1.3]. This includes $\bar{\mathbb{Q}}$, which the manuscript above does not cover.

**Scope.** This implies the corresponding assertion for the full group $G_K$ [@pop2003, Introduction, Remarks]. It fails for $\mathrm{td}(K|k) = 1$, where [$G^\ell_K$ does not depend on $K$](function-fields-of-curves-have-free-galois-groups.md).

**Status of the source.** The source is a 2003 manuscript on arXiv, whose page lists no journal reference as of November 2026.
