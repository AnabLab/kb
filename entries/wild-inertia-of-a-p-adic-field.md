---
title: Free wild inertia and its intrinsic characterization for p-adic fields
kind: theorem
summary: 'For a p-adic field, wild inertia is a free pro-p group that is not finitely generated. It is the largest closed normal pro-p subgroup of the absolute Galois group; tame inertia is the largest closed normal procyclic subgroup after quotienting by wild inertia.'
tags: [local-fields, galois-groups, pro-p-groups, ramification]
---

**Setting.** Let $K/\mathbb{Q}_p$ be finite, with residue field $k$. Inside a fixed algebraic closure, let $K^{\mathrm{ur}}$ and $K^{\mathrm{tr}}$ be the maximal unramified and tamely ramified extensions. Put $G_K=\mathrm{Gal}(\bar K/K)$, $I_K=\mathrm{Gal}(\bar K/K^{\mathrm{ur}})$ and $P_K=\mathrm{Gal}(\bar K/K^{\mathrm{tr}})$.

**Structure.** Wild inertia $P_K$ is the unique pro-$p$ Sylow subgroup of $I_K$, and $\operatorname{cd}_p(P_K)=1$ [@minamidetsujimura2026quasifinite, Lemma 1.2 and its proof, p. 13]. It is a free pro-$p$ group that is not topologically finitely generated, and
$$
I_K/P_K\cong\prod_{\ell\ne p}\mathbb{Z}_\ell(1)
$$
as a $G_k$-module [@minamidetsujimura2026quasifinite, Proposition 1.5(i)–(ii), p. 14, and proof, p. 15]. The twist denotes the natural action on prime-to-$p$ roots of unity.

**Intrinsic characterization.** The prime $p$ is the unique prime $\ell$ for which $G_K$ has a nontrivial closed normal pro-$\ell$ subgroup [@minamidetsujimura2026quasifinite, proof of Proposition 1.6, p. 16]. Moreover, $P_K$ is the largest closed normal pro-$p$ subgroup of $G_K$, and $I_K/P_K$ is the largest closed normal procyclic subgroup of $G_K/P_K$ [@minamidetsujimura2026quasifinite, proof of Proposition 1.7, p. 16]. The source's residue-field hypothesis holds because $k$ is finite [@minamidetsujimura2026quasifinite, Remark 1.7.1(i), pp. 16–17].

**Role.** These descriptions make wild inertia intrinsic in the [local Galois invariants](local-field-galois-invariants.md) and supply the pro-$p$ input to the lemma in the [Frobenius-shift lifting problem](frobenius-shift-lifting-problem.md). They do not decide that lifting problem.

**Source version.** Locations refer to the 77-page author manuscript linked from Tsujimura's homepage, not the differently paginated 2023 RIMS-1973 version.
