**Hypothesis.** A characteristic-zero field $k$ is *Kummer-faithful* if, for every finite extension $L/k$ and semi-abelian variety $A/L$,
$$\bigcap_{n\geq 1} nA(L)=\{0\}.$$
Equivalently, the full Kummer map on $A(L)$ is injective. Every [sub-$p$-adic field](mochizuki-hom-theorem-for-hyperbolic-curves.md) satisfies this condition [@mochizuki2015, author's copy, Definition 1.5 and Remark 1.5.4 (i)].

**Theorem.** Let $X/k$ be smooth, proper and geometrically connected of genus at least $2$, with $k$ Kummer-faithful. Put $K=k(X)$ and choose an algebraic closure $\bar k$ of $k$. There is a group-theoretic algorithm reconstructing the field $K$ from the extension
$$1\longrightarrow G_{K\bar k}\longrightarrow G_K\longrightarrow G_k\longrightarrow 1.$$
It is functorial for open injective homomorphisms of these extensions [@mochizuki2015, author's copy, Theorem 1.11].

**Algorithm.** The theorem gives four stages:

1. Recognize the cyclotomic characters up to power-equivalence from the geometric abelianization.
2. Recover genera and inertia groups $I_x$ of geometric points $x\in X(\bar k)$, hence decomposition groups $D_x=N_{G_K}(I_x)$, with compatible choices of prolongations.
3. Synchronize inertia with the geometric cyclotomic module and recognize principal divisors cohomologically.
4. Recover $K^\times$ as the subgroup generated by principal-divisor classes whose restriction to some $D_x$ vanishes; reconstruct addition using valuations and evaluation at points [@mochizuki2015, author's copy, Theorem 1.11 (a)–(d) and Proposition 1.3].

Step (b) has an erratum: its cyclic-cover test allows ramification at other points too [@mochizuki2019topicsiiicorrections, item (2)].

**Scope.** The geometric kernel is part of the input. Over number fields or finite extensions of $\mathbb Q_p$, it can itself be reconstructed [@mochizuki2015, author's copy, Remark 1.11.1].
