**Data.** For a quasicompact quasiseparated scheme $X$, the Barwick–Glasman–Haine category $\operatorname{Gal}(X)$ has geometric points as objects. A morphism $x\to y$ is a lift of $y$ to the strict localization $X_{(x)}$. Thus the underlying point of $x$ lies in the closure of that of $y$, and the automorphism group at $x$ is $G_{\kappa(x_0)}$, where $x_0$ is its underlying scheme point [@barwickglasmanhaine2020, Construction 0.0.1, p. 6]. This is distinct from the category of finite étale covers [@barwickglasmanhaine2020, §12.1, footnote 34, p. 188].

Retain the canonical profinite topology: its morphism-space topology is generated by requiring a geometric specialization to lie above a specified specialization between points finite over their images [@barwickglasmanhaine2020, Construction 0.0.2, p. 6]. For a $k$-scheme also retain $\operatorname{Gal}(X)\to BG_k$, where $BG_k$ is the one-object category of $G_k$.

**Local model.** For a discrete valuation ring $A$ with fraction field $F$ and residue field $\kappa$, the category is encoded by
$$
BG_\kappa\longleftarrow BD_A\longrightarrow BG_F,
$$
where $D_A\subseteq G_F$ is a decomposition group and $\ker(D_A\to G_\kappa)$ is its inertia group [@barwickglasmanhaine2020, Example 12.2.2, p. 190].

**Reconstruction theorem.** Let $k$ be finitely generated over $\mathbb Q$, and let $X,Y$ be normal finite-type $k$-schemes. The natural functor from the discrete groupoid $\operatorname{Hom}_k(X,Y)$ to the groupoid of continuous functors
$$
\operatorname{Gal}(X)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Gal}(Y)
\quad\text{over }BG_k
$$
preserving minimal objects is an equivalence. Morphisms in the latter groupoid are natural isomorphisms over $BG_k$. Minimal objects lie over closed scheme points. In particular, an equivalence of these topological categories over $BG_k$ determines a unique $k$-isomorphism $X\simeq Y$ [@barwickglasmanhaine2020, §14.4.3 and Theorem 14.4.7, pp. 232–233]. Equivalently, retain the geometric category $\operatorname{Gal}(X_{\bar k})$ with its continuous $G_k$-action [@barwickglasmanhaine2020, §14.4.8, p. 233].

**Mechanism.** Exodromy reconstructs the entire étale topos from $\operatorname{Gal}(X)$ [@barwickglasmanhaine2020, Theorem 12.1.6, p. 189]. The scheme-reconstruction step uses Voevodsky's theorem [@barwickglasmanhaine2020, Theorem 14.4.6 and the discussion preceding Theorem 14.4.7, pp. 232–233]. The [topos entry](etale-topos-reconstruction-over-sub-p-adic-fields.md) treats broader base fields and nonnormal schemes after localization. Here the retained invariant includes specialization morphisms and their topology as well as the residue-field groups.
