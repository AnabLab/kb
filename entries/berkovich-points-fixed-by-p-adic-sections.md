**Setting.** Let $K$ be a complete discretely valued field of characteristic $0$ whose residue field is algebraic over $\mathbb{F}_p$. Let $\Omega$ be the $p$-adic completion of $\bar K$, $\Sigma$ a set of primes containing $p$, and $\ell \in \Sigma \setminus \{p\}$. Let $H \subseteq G_K$ be a closed subgroup such that $H \cap I_K$ surjects onto $\mathbb{Z}_\ell$, where $I_K$ is the inertia group, and write $\Omega^H$ for the subfield of $\Omega$ fixed by $H$ [@mochizukitsujimura2024, Introduction, Theorem F, p. 7].

Let $X$ be a proper [hyperbolic curve](hyperbolic-curve.md) over $K$ that is defined over a finite extension of $\mathbb{Q}_p$ contained in $K$. Let $\tilde X \to X$ be a universal geometrically pro-$\Sigma$ covering, $\Pi^{(\Sigma)}_X = \mathrm{Gal}(\tilde X/X)$, and $\tilde X^{\mathrm{an}}$ the topological pro-Berkovich space, the inverse limit of the spaces underlying the Berkovich spaces of the finite subcoverings of $\tilde X$ [@mochizukitsujimura2024, Introduction, Theorem F (i), pp. 7–8]. The group $\Pi^{(\Sigma)}_X$ acts on $\tilde X^{\mathrm{an}}$. The points of $\tilde X^{\mathrm{an}}$ are the valuations $v$ of the function field of $\tilde X$ with $\mathcal{O}_v \cap K = \mathcal{O}_K$ whose maximal ideal is the only prime ideal of $\mathcal{O}_v$ containing $p$ [@mochizukitsujimura2024, Definition 2.2 (i), p. 30, and Proposition 2.3 (vii), p. 38]. The points of type 1 are those given by points of $\tilde X(\Omega)$ [@mochizukitsujimura2024, Definition 3.1 (i), p. 73].

**Theorem (Mochizuki–Tsujimura, preprint).** In this setting, let $s : H \to \Pi^{(\Sigma)}_X$ be a section of the restriction to $H$ of $\Pi^{(\Sigma)}_X \to G_K$.

1. At most one point of $\tilde X^{\mathrm{an}}$ is fixed by $s(H)$. If the $\ell$-adic cyclotomic character of $K$ has open image on $H$, then exactly one is.
2. The section $s$ comes from an $\Omega^H$-rational point of $X$ if and only if its image is, in the source's words, a maximal stabilizer in $\Pi^{(\Sigma)}_X \times_{G_K} H$ of a point of $\tilde X^{\mathrm{an}}$.

This is [@mochizukitsujimura2024, Introduction, Theorem F (i), pp. 7–8]. Existence holds without resolution of nonsingularities [@mochizukitsujimura2024, Proposition 2.3 (xi), p. 39]. Uniqueness uses [resolution of nonsingularities](resolution-of-nonsingularities.md), which holds here by the theorem in that entry, through the existence of stable models of covers that dominate a given model [@mochizukitsujimura2024, proof of Proposition 2.4 (vii), p. 54].

**Dominant maps.** Let $Y, Z$ be hyperbolic curves over $K$, not necessarily proper, each defined over a finite extension of $\mathbb{Q}_p$ contained in $K$. Let $f : Y \to Z$ be a dominant morphism over $K$, and suppose that the $\ell$-adic cyclotomic character has open image on $H$. Let $s_Y$ and $s_Z$ be sections over $H$ of the geometrically pro-$\Sigma$ fundamental groups of $Y$ and $Z$ such that $f$ maps $s_Y$ to $s_Z$ up to conjugation. Then $s_Y$ comes from an $\Omega^H$-rational point of $Y$ if and only if $s_Z$ comes from an $\Omega^H$-rational point of $Z$ [@mochizukitsujimura2024, Introduction, Theorem F (ii), p. 8, and Proposition 3.5 (iii), p. 79].

**Over a $p$-adic field.** This is checked here. Let $K$ be a finite extension of $\mathbb{Q}_p$ with residue field of order $q$, and $H = G_K$. The hypotheses hold:

- *Inertia.* Let $\pi$ be a uniformizer. The maximal unramified extension $K^{\mathrm{ur}}$ contains the $\ell^n$-th roots of unity, and $K^{\mathrm{ur}}(\pi^{1/\ell^n})$ is cyclic of degree $\ell^n$ over it, because $x^{\ell^n} - \pi$ is Eisenstein. So $I_K = G_{K^{\mathrm{ur}}}$ surjects onto $\mathbb{Z}/\ell^n$ compatibly in $n$, hence onto $\mathbb{Z}_\ell$.
- *Cyclotomic character.* Since $\ell \neq p$, it is unramified, with Frobenius acting by $q$. Its image is the closed subgroup of $\mathbb{Z}_\ell^\times$ generated by $q$. This subgroup is infinite because $q > 1$, and every closed infinite subgroup of $\mathbb{Z}_\ell^\times \cong (\text{finite}) \times \mathbb{Z}_\ell$ is open.

So every section of $\Pi^{(\Sigma)}_X \to G_K$, for a proper hyperbolic curve $X$ over $K$, fixes exactly one point of $\tilde X^{\mathrm{an}}$. By the [Ax–Sen–Tate fixed-field theorem](ax-sen-tate-fixed-field-theorem.md), $\Omega^{G_K}=K$ [@tate1967pdivisible, §3, Theorem 1, p. 176], so part 2 characterizes the sections that come from $K$-rational points.

**Comparison with Pop–Stix.** This is checked here; Mochizuki and Tsujimura do not compare the two results. Take $K$ finite over $\mathbb{Q}_p$, $\Sigma$ the set of all primes and $X$ proper. Then the function field of $\tilde X$ is the field $\tilde K$ of [the valuative section theorem](valuative-section-theorem-for-p-adic-curves.md). A point of $\tilde X^{\mathrm{an}}$ fixed by $s(G_K)$ is a valuation $\tilde w$ of $\tilde K$ prolonging the valuation of $K$, with $s(G_K)$ in its decomposition group. So existence in part 1 gives Pop and Stix's theorem for proper curves [@popstix2017, Theorem 26, p. 18]. Uniqueness says that $\tilde w$ is unique among valuations whose maximal ideal is the only prime ideal containing $p$. A refinement $\tilde v_y \circ \tilde\alpha$ of type 2v is not of this kind, since the maximal ideal of $\tilde\alpha$ is a smaller prime ideal containing $p$. This matches the two cases of [@popstix2017, Corollary 41, p. 25], which resolution of nonsingularities makes unconditional.

**Status.** For $K$ finite over $\mathbb{Q}_p$ and $\Sigma$ the set of all primes, part 2 recasts the $p$-adic section conjecture for proper $X$ as the statement that the image of every section is a maximal stabilizer. It does not prove it. The paper is a preprint, cited in its version of 30 March 2024. Mochizuki and Tsujimura remark that a similar result to the statement on dominant maps holds over number fields, by combining it with another theorem, but do not discuss it in detail [@mochizukitsujimura2024, Introduction, p. 8].
