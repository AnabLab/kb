**Question.** Is there a continuous representation $W : G_{\mathbb{Q}_3} \to \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Q}_3)$ which is Hodge–Tate but not Aut-intrinsically Hodge–Tate? Equivalently, can $W$ be Hodge–Tate while $W \circ \theta$ is not, for some continuous automorphism $\theta$ of $G_{\mathbb{Q}_3}$?

This specializes Hoshi's question for a given $p$-adic field to $\mathbb{Q}_3$ [@hoshi2024, Introduction, p. 2]. His Theorem B on that page constructs examples over some finite extension of $\mathbb{Q}_p$ for each odd $p$; it does not prescribe that extension to be $\mathbb{Q}_3$.
