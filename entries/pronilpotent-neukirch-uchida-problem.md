**Question.** For a number field $K$, let $G_K^{\mathrm{nil}}$ be the maximal pronilpotent quotient of $G_K$, and $G_K(\ell)$ its maximal pro-$\ell$ quotient. If $K, L$ are number fields with $G_K^{\mathrm{nil}} \cong G_L^{\mathrm{nil}}$, must $K \cong L$? Since $G_K^{\mathrm{nil}} = \prod_\ell G_K(\ell)$ with characteristic factors ([product decomposition](relative-pronilpotent-neukirch-uchida.md)), this asks the following. If $G_K(\ell) \cong G_L(\ell)$ for every prime $\ell$, by isomorphisms that need not be related to each other, must $K \cong L$? One can also ask for the functorial form, in which every isomorphism $G_K^{\mathrm{nil}} \to G_L^{\mathrm{nil}}$ comes from a unique field isomorphism.

**A withdrawn claim.** Karshon and Shusterman state that the maximal pronilpotent quotient does not determine $K$, citing Golich and McReynolds [@karshonshusterman2026, Abstract and §1, p. 1]. The cited claim gives non-isomorphic number fields $K_1, K_2$ such that, for every $j$, the quotients of $G_{K_1}$ and $G_{K_2}$ by the $j$-th terms of their lower central series are isomorphic, compatibly with the corresponding quotient of $G_{\mathbb Q}$ [@golichmcreynolds2023diamonds, Corollary 1.5, p. 4]. The fields are those of Prasad, which have the same abelian quotients [@golichmcreynolds2023diamonds, Remark 3, p. 4]. In October 2026 the authors withdrew the paper, commenting that "Theorem 1.9 is false as stated" and that they "no longer have an examples" [@golichmcreynolds2023diamonds, arXiv v3 listing]. No counterexample is recorded here.

**Known.**

- *Neighbouring quotients.* The class-2 nilpotent quotient, a quotient of $G_K^{\mathrm{nil}}$, [does not determine $K$](two-step-nilpotent-not-anabelian.md). The maximal prosupersolvable quotient, of which $G_K^{\mathrm{nil}}$ is a quotient, [does](prosupersolvable-quotient-determines-number-field.md), and so does the maximal [pronilpotent-by-abelian quotient](pro-l-by-cyclotomic-neukirch-uchida.md). The Sylow subgroups of $G_K$ itself, which are different data, [do not](sylow-subgroups-do-not-determine-number-field.md).
- *Pro-$\ell$ quotients.* [Shimizu's theorem](pro-c-neukirch-uchida.md) does not apply to $G_K(\ell)$, because it needs the set of primes of the class to have positive density. Shimizu conjectures that every isomorphism of maximal pro-$\ell$ quotients comes from a unique field isomorphism, provided $\ell = 2$ or one of the fields has a complex place [@shimizu2023proc, Conjecture 7.12, p. 34, and Remark 7.15 (i), p. 35]. The case $\ell = 2$ would answer the question positively, using $G_K(2)$ alone.
- *Relative form (proved here).* An isomorphism $G_K^{\mathrm{nil}} \to G_L^{\mathrm{nil}}$ that commutes with restriction to $\mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb Q^{\mathrm{ab}}/\mathbb Q)$ forces $K \cong L$, by [the relative theorem](relative-pronilpotent-neukirch-uchida.md), which rests on Shimizu's relative theorem for pro-$2$ quotients. In particular, the pronilpotent analogue of the withdrawn claim cannot hold. That would be a single isomorphism $G_{K_1}^{\mathrm{nil}} \to G_{K_2}^{\mathrm{nil}}$ compatible with the maps to $G_{\mathbb Q}^{\mathrm{nil}}$, hence with those to $\mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb Q^{\mathrm{ab}}/\mathbb Q)$.
- *Functorial form (proved here).* It fails for every $K$ with $\mathrm{Aut}(K) \ne 1$. Indeed $\mathrm{Out}(G_K^{\mathrm{nil}})$ contains $\prod_\ell \mathrm{Aut}(K)$, while only the diagonal copy of $\mathrm{Aut}(K)$ comes from field automorphisms ([non-functoriality](relative-pronilpotent-neukirch-uchida.md)). For $K = \mathbb Q$ the functorial form asks whether every automorphism of $G_{\mathbb Q}(\ell)$ is inner, for every $\ell$.

**Status.** The weak form, and the functorial form for fields without automorphisms, are open as far as is known here. A literature search in November 2026 found no answer other than the withdrawn claim, but that search was not exhaustive.
