**Statement.** Let $K|k$ be a function field over an algebraically closed field with $\mathrm{char}(k) \ne \ell$ and $\mathrm{td}(K|k) \ge 2$. By results of Pop and Topaz, there are concrete uniform group-theoretic recipes that, given $\Pi^c_K$, recover the minimized quasi-divisorial groups $T^1_v \subset Z^1_v \subset \Pi^a_K$, and so the set $\mathrm{In.q.div}^1(K)$ of minimized quasi-divisorial inertia. Let $\overline{\mathrm{Isom}}^c(\Pi^a_K, \Pi^a_L)$ be the set of abelianizations of isomorphisms $\Pi^c_K \to \Pi^c_L$, modulo multiplication by $\ell$-adic units. Each of its elements carries $\mathrm{In.q.div}^1(K)$ onto $\mathrm{In.q.div}^1(L)$ [@pop2018, §1].

**Mod-$\ell$ version.** Minimized inertia and decomposition subgroups of $G^a_K$ associated to Parshin chains of quasi-divisorial valuations can be determined from the group-theoretic structure of $G^c_K$. The mod-$\ell$ theory of commuting-liftable pairs is the additional input [@topaz2016, §1.3].

**Limit.** As of 2015, this was the best the local theory gave over an arbitrary algebraically closed field [@topaz2016, §1.1]. See [the problem of recovering divisorial inertia](divisorial-inertia-recovery-problem.md).

**Attribution.** The recipes are attributed through [@pop2018, §1] and [@topaz2016, §1.3]. Pop's original papers on this have not been opened for this entry.
