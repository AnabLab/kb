Let $\Sigma$ be a nonempty set of primes, and $\mathcal G,\mathcal H$ semi-graphs of anabelioids of *pro-$\Sigma$ PSC-type*: pro-$\Sigma$ completions of graphs of Galois categories associated to pointed stable curves over algebraically closed fields of characteristic outside $\Sigma$. Vertices represent components, closed edges nodes, and open edges cusps. Write $\Pi_{\mathcal G}$ for the associated pro-$\Sigma$ PSC-fundamental group [@mochizuki2007combinatorial, Definition 1.1 (i)–(ii), pp. 458–459].

For each connected finite $\Pi_{\mathcal G}$-cover $\mathcal G'$, set $M_{\mathcal G'}=\Pi_{\mathcal G'}^{\mathrm{ab}}$. Its closed submodules $M_{\mathcal G'}^{\mathrm{edge}}$ and $M_{\mathcal G'}^{\mathrm{vert}}$ are generated by images of edge and vertex subgroups, respectively [@mochizuki2007combinatorial, Definition 1.1 (ii), p. 459].

**Theorem (Mochizuki).** A continuous isomorphism $\alpha:\Pi_{\mathcal G}\xrightarrow{\sim}\Pi_{\mathcal H}$ is induced by an isomorphism $\mathcal G\cong\mathcal H$ if and only if, on every pair of corresponding finite covers, it preserves both submodules:
$$
\alpha^{\mathrm{ab}}(M_{\mathcal G'}^{\mathrm{edge}})=M_{\mathcal H'}^{\mathrm{edge}},\qquad
\alpha^{\mathrm{ab}}(M_{\mathcal G'}^{\mathrm{vert}})=M_{\mathcal H'}^{\mathrm{vert}}.
$$
Thus the filtrations throughout the covering tower are input data [@mochizuki2007combinatorial, Definition 1.4 (i), (iii), pp. 462–463; Theorem 1.6 (ii), p. 465].

**Corrections.** Use the author's May 2020 replacements for the subgroup-recovery arguments in the proof [@mochizuki2020combinatorialcomments, item (4), pp. 1–4]. Compare [reconstruction from unipotent groupoids](unipotent-groupoid-reconstruction-of-reduction-graphs.md).
