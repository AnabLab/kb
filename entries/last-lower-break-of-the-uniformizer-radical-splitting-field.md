**Setting.** Let $p$ be any prime, $K/\mathbb Q_p$ finite with $v_K(K^\times)=\mathbb Z$, $e=v_K(p)$, $\pi$ a uniformizer and $s\ge1$. Fix a root $\pi_s$ of $X^{p^s}-\pi$ and a primitive $p^s$th root of unity $\zeta$, put $\zeta_{p^j}=\zeta^{p^{s-j}}$, and set
$$F=K(\pi_s),\qquad N=F(\zeta).$$
So $N$ is the [splitting field](ramification-of-uniformizer-radical-splitting-fields.md) of $X^{p^s}-\pi$; the fields $F$ and $N$ are $K_s$ and $N_s$ of the [prismatic entry](prismatic-ramification-bound-for-mod-p-cohomology.md). For a finite extension $M'/M$ write $e_{M'/M}$ for its ramification index and $d_{M'/M}=v_{M'}(\mathfrak D_{M'/M})$ for its different exponent, with $v_{M'}$ normalized. Put $E=e_{N/K}$. For a finite Galois extension $M'/M$ with group $G$, use the numbering of [@coupek2022crystalline, §5.1, pp. 27–28]: $G_{(\lambda)}$ is the set of $g$ with $v_{M'}(gx-x)\ge\lambda$ for all $x\in\mathcal O_{M'}$, $\varphi_{M'/M}(t)=\int_0^t dx/[G_{(1)}:G_{(x)}]$, $\lambda_{M'/M}$ is the infimum of the $\lambda\ge0$ with $G_{(\lambda)}=1$, and $\mu_{M'/M}=\varphi_{M'/M}(\lambda_{M'/M})$. These are Serre's groups with the index shifted by one [@coupek2022crystalline, Remark 5.1, p. 28].

**Theorem (proved here).**
$$\lambda_{N/K}=E\Bigl(\frac{e}{p-1}+\frac1{p^s}\Bigr)-d_{N/F}.$$

(a) The first term is $v_N(\sigma\pi_s-\pi_s)$ for any $\sigma\in\operatorname{Gal}(N/K)$ with $\sigma\pi_s=\zeta_p\pi_s$. So the inequality $\lambda_{N/K}\ge E(e/(p-1)+1/p^s)$ of [@coupek2022crystalline, proof of Theorem 5.15, p. 34] holds if and only if $N/F$ is unramified, which happens if and only if $K(\zeta)/K$ is unramified. Otherwise it fails by $d_{N/F}\ge e_{N/F}-1\ge1$.

(b) Always $\lambda_{N/K}\le1+p^se\,e_{N/F}/(p-1)$, with equality if and only if $N/F$ is tamely ramified. For odd $p$ this happens if and only if $K(\zeta)/K(\zeta_p)$ is unramified, for instance when $s=1$, and then
$$\lambda_{N/K}=1+\frac{p^se}{\gcd(p-1,e)}.$$
For $p=2$, $N/F$ is a $2$-extension, so tame means unramified; this is case (a), and the formula holds with $\gcd(1,e)=1$.

**Proof of the identity.** Let $G=\operatorname{Gal}(N/K)$. For $g\ne1$ put $i(g)=\min_{x\in\mathcal O_N}v_N(gx-x)$. It is $0$ outside the inertia group $I=G_{(1)}$, whose order is $E$ [@milne2020ant, Theorem 7.58(a), p. 131]. By definition $G_{(x)}=\{1\}\cup\{g\ne1:i(g)\ge x\}$. As $i$ is integer-valued, $G_{(x)}=I$ for $0<x\le1$, and $\lambda_{N/K}=\max_{g\ne1}i(g)$. Hence, whatever the residue degree of $N/K$,
$$E\,\varphi_{N/K}(t)=\int_0^t|G_{(x)}|\,dx=t+\sum_{g\ne1}\min\{t,i(g)\}.$$
For $t\ge\lambda_{N/K}$ the sum is $\sum_{g\ne1}i(g)=d_{N/K}$, by the formula for the [different as a sum of displacements](discriminants-recover-ramification-filtrations.md). Taking $t=\lambda_{N/K}$ gives
$$\lambda_{N/K}=E\mu_{N/K}-d_{N/K}.\tag{1}$$
The [radical splitting-field theorem](ramification-of-uniformizer-radical-splitting-fields.md) gives the last Serre upper break $e(s+1/(p-1))$, so $\mu_{N/K}=1+es+e/(p-1)$. Differents multiply in towers [@sutherland2025different, Proposition 12.28, p. 10], so $d_{N/K}=d_{N/F}+e_{N/F}\,d_{F/K}$. As $X^{p^s}-\pi$ is Eisenstein, $F/K$ is totally ramified of degree $p^s$, $\pi_s$ is a uniformizer, and $\mathcal O_F=\mathcal O_K[\pi_s]$ [@milne2020ant, Proposition 7.55, p. 129; Remark 7.56, p. 130]. The derivative formula [@sutherland2025different, Proposition 12.24, p. 9] then gives $d_{F/K}=v_F(p^s\pi_s^{p^s-1})=p^ses+p^s-1$. Substituting into (1) with $E=p^se_{N/F}$,
$$\lambda_{N/K}=e_{N/F}\Bigl(p^s\bigl(1+es+\tfrac{e}{p-1}\bigr)-p^ses-p^s+1\Bigr)-d_{N/F}=e_{N/F}\Bigl(\frac{p^se}{p-1}+1\Bigr)-d_{N/F},$$
which is the identity. An element $\sigma$ as in (a) exists because $G$ acts transitively on the roots of the irreducible polynomial $X^{p^s}-\pi$, and $v_N(\sigma\pi_s-\pi_s)=v_N(\zeta_p-1)+v_N(\pi_s)=Ee/(p-1)+E/p^s$, since $v_K(\zeta_p-1)=e/(p-1)$ by the radical splitting-field entry.

**Lemma 1 (proved here).** $N/F$ is unramified if and only if $K(\zeta)/K$ is unramified.

*Proof.* If $K(\zeta)/K$ is unramified, it lies in $K(\mu_m)$ for some $m$ prime to $p$, so $N=F\cdot K(\zeta)$ lies in $F(\mu_m)$, which is unramified over $F$ [@milne2020ant, Corollaries 7.51 and 7.52, p. 128].

Conversely, let $N/F$ be unramified, so $E=p^s$. Let $T$ be the fixed field of $I$. Then $T/K$ is unramified and $[N:T]=p^s$ [@milne2020ant, Theorem 7.58(a), p. 131]. As $\pi$ is a uniformizer of $T$, the polynomial $X^{p^s}-\pi$ is Eisenstein over $T$, so $N=T(\pi_s)$, $\pi_s$ is a uniformizer of $N$, and $\mathcal O_N=\mathcal O_T[\pi_s]$. For $\gamma\in I=\operatorname{Gal}(N/T)$ write $\gamma\pi_s=\zeta^{a(\gamma)}\pi_s$ and $\gamma\zeta=\zeta^{c(\gamma)}$. The group $I$, of order $p^s$, acts transitively on the $p^s$ roots, so $\gamma\mapsto a(\gamma)\in\mathbb Z/p^s$ is a bijection. Every displacement $\gamma x-x$ with $x\in\mathcal O_T[\pi_s]$ is divisible by $\gamma\pi_s-\pi_s$, so $i(\gamma)=v_N(\zeta^{a(\gamma)}-1)+1$. For a primitive $p^k$th root of unity $\xi$ one has $v_N(\xi-1)=p^se/(p^{k-1}(p-1))$, because $v_N=p^sv_K$ on $K$.

Let $t\le s$ be largest with $\zeta_{p^t}\in T$. Then $t\ge1$: for odd $p$, $[T(\zeta_p):T]$ divides both $p-1$ and $[N:T]=p^s$, and $\zeta_2=-1$. Suppose $t<s$.

(i) Let $\gamma\ne1$ fix $\pi_t=\pi_s^{p^{s-t}}$. Then $p^t$ divides $a(\gamma)$, so $\zeta^{a(\gamma)}$ has order $p^k$ with $1\le k\le s-t$, and $i(\gamma)>p^se/(p^{k-1}(p-1))$. If $c(\gamma)\ne1$, let $r<s$ be the $p$-adic valuation of $c(\gamma)-1$. Then $\gamma\zeta/\zeta$ is a primitive $p^{s-r}$th root of unity, and $i(\gamma)\le v_N(\gamma\zeta-\zeta)=p^se/(p^{s-r-1}(p-1))$. Comparing the two bounds gives $s-r<k$, so $r>s-k\ge t$. In every case $c(\gamma)\equiv1$ modulo $p^{t+1}$, so $\gamma$ fixes $\zeta_{p^{t+1}}$. Hence $\zeta_{p^{t+1}}\in T(\pi_t)$.

(ii) Since $\mu_{p^t}\subset T$ and $X^{p^t}-\pi$ is Eisenstein, $T(\pi_t)/T$ is Galois of degree $p^t$, and $\tau\mapsto\tau\pi_t/\pi_t$ is an isomorphism from its group onto $\mu_{p^t}$. Let $\tau$ be the generator with $\tau\pi_t=\zeta_{p^t}\pi_t$, and put $y=\zeta_{p^{t+1}}$ and $\pi_1=\pi_t^{p^{t-1}}$. Then $\tau\pi_1=\zeta_p\pi_1$, and $\tau y=\zeta_p^jy$ for some $j$ because $y^p\in T$. As $y\notin T$, $j\not\equiv0$ modulo $p$. Choose $k$ with $jk\equiv1$ modulo $p$. Then $z=y^k/\pi_1$ is fixed by $\tau$, so $z\in T$, and $\pi=\pi_1^p=\zeta_{p^t}^kz^{-p}$ has valuation in $p\mathbb Z$, a contradiction. So $t=s$, and $K(\zeta)\subseteq T$ is unramified over $K$. $\square$

**Lemma 2 (proved here).** Let $p$ be odd and $M/\mathbb Q_p$ finite. Then $e_{M(\zeta_p)/M}=(p-1)/\gcd(p-1,v_M(p))$.

*Proof.* From $p=\prod_{h=1}^{p-1}(1-\zeta_p^h)$ and $(1-\zeta_p^h)/(1-\zeta_p)\equiv h$ modulo $1-\zeta_p$, Wilson's theorem gives $-p=(1-\zeta_p)^{p-1}w$ with $w\equiv1$ modulo $1-\zeta_p$. By Hensel's lemma $w=c^{p-1}$ with $c\in\mathbb Q_p(\zeta_p)$, so $\vartheta=(1-\zeta_p)c$ is a root of the Eisenstein polynomial $X^{p-1}+p$. Since $\Phi_p(X+1)$ is Eisenstein as well, comparing degrees gives $\mathbb Q_p(\zeta_p)=\mathbb Q_p(\vartheta)$, so $M(\zeta_p)=M(\vartheta)$. Put $n=p-1$, $v=v_M(p)$ and $g=\gcd(n,v)$, and write $-p=\varpi^vu$ with $\varpi$ a uniformizer and $u$ a unit of $M$. Choose $a$ with $a^{n/g}=\varpi^{v/g}$ and $b$ with $b^n=u$. Then $(ab)^n=-p$, so $\vartheta\in M(a,b)$, since $\mu_n\subset\mathbb Q_p$. The minimal polynomial $f$ of $b$ divides $X^n-u$, whose reduction is separable, so $f'(b)$ is a unit. The different of $M(b)/M$ divides $f'(b)$ [@sutherland2025different, Proposition 12.26, p. 9], so $M(b)/M$ is unramified [@sutherland2025different, Theorem 12.19, p. 7]. Moreover $[M(a,b):M(b)]\le n/g$. So $e_{M(\vartheta)/M}\le n/g$. Conversely, $v_M(\vartheta)=v/n=(v/g)/(n/g)$ in lowest terms, so $n/g$ divides $e_{M(\vartheta)/M}$. $\square$

**Proof of (a) and (b).** By the identity, the inequality in (a) holds exactly when $d_{N/F}=0$, that is, when $N/F$ is unramified [@sutherland2025different, Theorem 12.19, p. 7]; Lemma 1 translates this. For (b), $e_{N/F}-1\le d_{N/F}$, with equality exactly in the tame case [@sutherland2025different, Theorem 12.27, p. 10], and the identity rewrites as $\lambda_{N/K}=1+p^se\,e_{N/F}/(p-1)-(d_{N/F}-e_{N/F}+1)$.

Let $p$ be odd and $K_1=K(\zeta_p)$. The degree of $F(\zeta_p)/F$ divides $p-1$, and $\operatorname{Gal}(N/F(\zeta_p))$ embeds in the kernel of $(\mathbb Z/p^s)^\times\to(\mathbb Z/p)^\times$, a $p$-group. So $N/F$ is tame if and only if $N/F(\zeta_p)$ is unramified, and then $e_{N/F}=e_{F(\zeta_p)/F}$. To apply Lemma 1 over $K_1$, put $g'=e_{K_1/K}$, which is prime to $p$. Write $\pi=u\varpi^{g'}$ with $\varpi$ a uniformizer and $u$ a unit of $K_1$, choose $h\ge1$ with $hg'=1+p^sm$, and put $\varpi'=u^h\varpi$. Then $\pi^h=\varpi'\,(\varpi^m)^{p^s}$, so $\pi_s^h\varpi^{-m}$ is a root of the Eisenstein polynomial $X^{p^s}-\varpi'$ in $K_1(\pi_s)$. Comparing degrees, $K_1(\pi_s)=F(\zeta_p)$ is generated over $K_1$ by a $p^s$th root of the uniformizer $\varpi'$, and $N$ is obtained from it by adjoining $\zeta$. Lemma 1 for $(K_1,\varpi',s)$ shows that $N/F(\zeta_p)$ is unramified if and only if $K(\zeta)/K_1$ is unramified. In that case Lemma 2 with $M=F$ and $v_F(p)=p^se$ gives $e_{N/F}=(p-1)/\gcd(p-1,e)$, as $p^s\equiv1$ modulo $p-1$. Substituting gives the formula. $\square$

**Checks.** For $p=3$, $K=\mathbb Q_3$, $\pi=3$ and $s=1$, (b) gives $\lambda_{N/K}=1+3=4$. This matches the direct computation in the [prismatic entry](prismatic-ramification-bound-for-mod-p-cohomology.md); the printed bound $5$ exceeds it by $e_{N/F}-1=1$.

A wild case, computed independently: $p=2$, $K=\mathbb Q_2$, $\pi=2$, $s=2$. Write $\varpi=\pi_2=2^{1/4}$ and $\iota=\zeta_4$, so $N=\mathbb Q_2(\varpi,\iota)$. Since $\mathbb Q_2(\iota)/\mathbb Q_2$ is ramified, Lemma 1 shows that $N/F$, of degree at most $2$, is ramified. So $N/K$ is totally ramified of degree $E=8$, with residue field $\mathbb F_2$ and dihedral group. The identity predicts $\lambda_{N/K}=8(1+1/4)-d_{N/F}$. Put $\zeta_8=(1+\iota)/\varpi^2$, $z=\zeta_8-1$ and $y=z/\varpi$. Then $v_N(z)=v_N(\varpi)=2$, and $(z+1)^4=-1$ gives $y^4+1=-3z^2-2z-2z^3$, of valuation $4$. Writing $y=1+\delta$, the expansion $y^4+1=2+4\delta+6\delta^2+4\delta^3+\delta^4$ forces $v_N(\delta)=1$, so $\delta$ is a uniformizer and $i(g)=v_N(gy-y)$. On representatives of the conjugacy classes:
- $\sigma:\varpi\mapsto-\varpi,\ \iota\mapsto\iota$ fixes $\zeta_8$, so $\sigma y-y=-2y$ and $i(\sigma)=8$;
- $\rho:\varpi\mapsto\iota\varpi,\ \iota\mapsto\iota$ sends $\zeta_8$ to $-\zeta_8$, so $\rho y-y=(\iota-1)y+2\iota/\varpi$ and $i(\rho)=4$;
- $\kappa:\varpi\mapsto\varpi,\ \iota\mapsto-\iota$ sends $\zeta_8$ to $\zeta_8^{-1}$, so $\kappa y-y=-\zeta_8^{-1}(\iota-1)/\varpi$ and $i(\kappa)=2$;
- $\kappa\rho:\varpi\mapsto-\iota\varpi,\ \iota\mapsto-\iota$ sends $\zeta_8$ to $-\zeta_8^{-1}$, so $\varpi(\kappa\rho y-y)=1-2\zeta_8-\zeta_8^2=-(2+4z+z^2)$ and $i(\kappa\rho)=2$.

So $\lambda_{N/K}=8$, and the identity requires $d_{N/F}=10-8=2$. Separately, the different formula gives $d_{N/K}=\sum_{g\ne1}i(g)=8+2\cdot4+4\cdot2=24$, so $d_{N/F}=24-2\,d_{F/K}=24-2\cdot11=2$, in agreement. Also $\varphi_{N/K}(8)=2+2\cdot\tfrac48+4\cdot\tfrac28=4=1+es+e/(p-1)$, as the radical splitting-field theorem predicts. The printed bound $10$ fails by $d_{N/F}=2>e_{N/F}-1$.

**Effect on the printed route.** The function $\varphi_{N/K}$ is concave with last slope $1/E$, so by the identity $\varphi_{N/K}(t)\le\mu_{N/K}+(t-\lambda_{N/K})/E=1+es+t/E-1/p^s+d_{N/F}/E$ for all $t\ge0$, with equality for $t\ge\lambda_{N/K}$. The printed proof of [@coupek2022crystalline, Theorem 5.15(2), p. 34] evaluates this bound at $t=Ea/p^s$ with $s=\alpha$, where $a$ and $\alpha$ are as in the prismatic entry. With the exact value it gives $1+e\alpha+(a-1)/p^\alpha+d_{N/F}/E$, so that route proves the upper cutoff of the prismatic entry only with $\beta$ replaced by $\beta+d_{N/F}/E$. The restriction argument recorded in that entry has no such loss.
