**Source and status.** Roe and Turturean's manuscript dated 26 July 2026 claims a presentation of $G_{\mathbb Q_2}$ with four generators, two word relations, and a condition that the normal closure of the wild generators is pro-$2$ [@roeturturean2026, Theorem 1.2, pp. 3–4]. Its project page states that it has not yet been externally peer reviewed. The claimed proof compares finite surjection counts and then reconstructs the profinite group [@roeturturean2026, §1.1, pp. 5–6].

**Relation to the open classification.** Extension to every finite $K/\mathbb Q_2$ is explicitly a further question [@roeturturean2026, §11, item 1, p. 67]. The full [dyadic classification](isomorphism-classes-of-p-adic-galois-groups.md) and [Frobenius-shift lifting](frobenius-shift-lifting-problem.md) therefore remain separate questions.
