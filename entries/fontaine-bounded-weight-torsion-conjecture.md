**Question (Fontaine).** Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, $T$ a finite free $\mathbb Z_p$-module with continuous $G_K$-action, and $r>0$ fixed. Suppose that, for every $n\ge1$, the module $T/p^nT$ is a quotient of two full stable lattices in a crystalline, respectively semistable, representation with Hodge–Tate weights in $\{0,\ldots,r\}$. Must $T\otimes\mathbb Q_p$ have the same property?

**Status.** Answered affirmatively by Liu. The question and its attribution to Fontaine are recorded in [@liu2007torsion, §1, Conjecture 1.0.1 and following paragraph, p. 633]. The [theorem entry](torsion-crystalline-quotients-detect-crystallinity.md) records the answer and the role of the common weight bound.
