---
title: The Milnor K-ring and its mod-l and divisible-free quotients
kind: definition
summary: 'The Milnor K-ring of a field $K$ is the tensor algebra of $K^\times$ modulo the Steinberg relations $x \otimes y$ for $x + y = 1$. Reconstruction results also use its mod-$\ell$ quotient and its quotient by infinitely divisible elements.'
tags: [milnor-k-theory, function-fields]
---

**Definition.** For a field $K$, the Milnor K-ring is

$$K^M_*(K) = T_*(K^\times) / \langle x \otimes y \mid x + y = 1 \rangle,$$

where $T_*(K^\times)$ is the tensor algebra of the $\mathbb{Z}$-module $K^\times$. In degree one, $K^M_1(K) = K^\times$, while the ring structure involves the additive structure of $K$ as well [@topaz2022, §1].

**Quotients.** The mod-$\ell$ Milnor K-ring is $k^M_*(K) = K^M_*(K)/\ell$, so that $k^M_1(K) = K^\times/\ell$ [@topaz2016, §2]. Bogomolov and Tschinkel work with the quotient $\bar K^M_*(K)$ by infinitely divisible elements [@bogomolovtschinkel2012, §2]. For a function field $K$ over an algebraically closed field $k$, one has $\bar K^M_1(K) = K^\times/k^\times$ [@topaz2016, §1.5].
