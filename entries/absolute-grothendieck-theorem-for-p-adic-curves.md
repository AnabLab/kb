For the question answered here, its history and the partial results that preceded this theorem, see the [absolute Grothendieck conjecture over $p$-adic fields](absolute-grothendieck-conjecture-for-p-adic-curves.md).

**Statement.** Let $k, k'$ be finite extensions of $\mathbb{Q}_p$ and $\mathbb{Q}_{p'}$, and let $X, X'$ be [hyperbolic curves](hyperbolic-curve.md) over $k, k'$. Let $\Sigma$ be a set of primes with at least two elements that contains $p$ and $p'$. Write $\Pi^{(\Sigma)}_X$ for the geometrically pro-$\Sigma$ fundamental group, the quotient of $\pi_1(X)$ by the kernel of the maximal pro-$\Sigma$ quotient of $\pi_1(X \otimes_k \bar k)$. When $\Sigma$ is the set of all primes, $\Pi^{(\Sigma)}_X = \pi_1(X)$. Then the natural map
$$\mathrm{Isom}(X, X') \to \mathrm{OutIsom}(\Pi^{(\Sigma)}_X, \Pi^{(\Sigma)}_{X'})$$
is bijective. Here $\mathrm{Isom}$ is the set of isomorphisms of schemes, with no base field fixed, and $\mathrm{OutIsom}$ is the set of isomorphisms of profinite groups modulo composition with inner automorphisms of the target [@mochizukitsujimura2024, Theorem 3.12, and Introduction, Theorem D]. Every isomorphism of schemes $X \to X'$ lies over an isomorphism between $k$ and $k'$, as one sees by considering the subgroups of the unit groups of $X$ and $X'$ whose union with $0$ is closed under addition [@mochizukitsujimura2024, proof of Theorem 3.12].

The same holds for the configuration spaces of $X$ and $X'$, with their profinite fundamental groups [@mochizukitsujimura2024, Theorem 3.13]. The proof combines Theorem 3.12 with the relative theorem of [Mochizuki](mochizuki-hom-theorem-for-hyperbolic-curves.md) [@mochizukitsujimura2024, proof of Theorem 3.13].

**What it answers.** Take $p' = p$ and $\Sigma$ the set of all primes, which has at least two elements and contains $p$. Then $\Pi^{(\Sigma)}$ is the full étale fundamental group, and the statement is the question exactly as the authors pose it [@mochizukitsujimura2024, Introduction, p. 5]. They call Theorem 3.12 a complete affirmative resolution of the absolute version in the geometrically pro-$\Sigma$ case, for every $\Sigma$ with at least two elements that contains the residue characteristic [@mochizukitsujimura2024, Remark 3.12.1, p. 91]. The geometrically pro-$p$ case is not covered. The relative version, over a fixed $G_k$, is [Mochizuki's Hom theorem](mochizuki-hom-theorem-for-hyperbolic-curves.md).

**Consequences for the base field.** These are checked here.

1. *$\pi_1(X)$ determines $k$.* If $X/k$ and $X'/k'$ are hyperbolic curves over finite extensions of $\mathbb{Q}_p$ and $\mathbb{Q}_{p'}$ with $\pi_1(X) \cong \pi_1(X')$, then $k \cong k'$. Indeed, take $\Sigma$ to be all primes. Then $\mathrm{OutIsom}$ is nonempty, so by the theorem there is an isomorphism of schemes $X \to X'$, and it lies over an isomorphism between $k$ and $k'$. For example, for odd $p$ the fields $K = \mathbb{Q}_p(\sqrt[p]{p})$ and $L = \mathbb{Q}_p(\sqrt[p]{p + p^2})$ are not isomorphic but have $G_K \cong G_L$ (see the examples in [the classification of $p$-adic Galois groups](isomorphism-classes-of-p-adic-galois-groups.md)). So $\pi_1(X) \not\cong \pi_1(Y)$ for every hyperbolic curve $X$ over $K$ and every hyperbolic curve $Y$ over $L$.
2. *Non-geometric automorphisms of $G_k$ do not lift.* Let $X$ be a hyperbolic curve over $k$, with $\Delta_X$ the kernel of $\pi_1(X) \to G_k$, and let $\psi$ be an automorphism of $\pi_1(X)$. Then $\psi(\Delta_X) = \Delta_X$ [@mochizuki2012, Corollary 2.8 (ii)], so $\psi$ induces an automorphism $\alpha$ of $G_k$. By the theorem, $\psi$ agrees up to an inner automorphism with the map induced by an automorphism $f$ of the scheme $X$, and $f$ lies over an automorphism $\sigma$ of $k$. The square formed by $f$, $\sigma$ and the structure morphisms commutes, so the map that $f$ induces on $\pi_1(X)$ induces on $G_k$ the map given by an extension of $\sigma$ to $\bar k$. Inner automorphisms of $\pi_1(X)$ induce inner automorphisms of $G_k$. Hence the class of $\alpha$ in $\mathrm{Out}(G_k)$ lies in the image of $\mathrm{Aut}(k)$. So no outer automorphism of $G_k$ outside that image, such as the outer automorphisms of $G_{\mathbb{Q}_p}$ for odd $p$ (see [generators and relations](local-galois-group-presentations.md)), lifts to an automorphism of $\pi_1(X)$. Compare [the field-theoretic subgroup of outer automorphisms](field-theoretic-subgroup-of-outer-automorphisms.md).

**Method.** The main input is [resolution of nonsingularities](resolution-of-nonsingularities.md) for arbitrary hyperbolic curves over $p$-adic fields, generalizing results of Tamagawa and of Lepage, the latter for Mumford curves [@mochizukitsujimura2024, Introduction, pp. 3–4, Theorem A]. With combinatorial anabelian geometry, the authors reconstruct from $\Pi^{(\Sigma)}_X$ the set of decomposition groups of closed points (see [the reconstruction of points from tempered fundamental groups](points-of-p-adic-curves-from-tempered-fundamental-groups.md)), and then apply Mochizuki's point-theoreticity result, recorded with the partial results in [the question's entry](absolute-grothendieck-conjecture-for-p-adic-curves.md) [@mochizukitsujimura2024, Introduction, pp. 6–7, and proof of Theorem 3.12].

**Status of the source.** The paper was issued as RIMS Preprint 1974 in June 2023, and a revised version dated 30 March 2024 is on Mochizuki's web page. This entry cites the revised version. No journal version was found in November 2026, but that search was not exhaustive.
