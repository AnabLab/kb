---
title: Reconstructing fields from their combinatorial geometry
kind: theorem
summary: For field extensions of transcendence degree at least $5$, the combinatorial geometry given by relative algebraic closure determines the field up to purely inseparable extension. This is due to Evans and Hrushovski for algebraically closed fields, and to Gismatullin in general.
tags: [function-fields]
---

**Statement.** For a field extension $K \subset L$, the algebraic combinatorial geometry $G(L/K)$ is obtained from the pregeometry of algebraic dependence over $K$ by identifying elements with the same relative algebraic closure over $K$. Suppose $L_1, L_2$ are perfect, $K_i$ is relatively algebraically closed in $L_i$, and the transcendence degrees are at least $5$. Then $G(L_1/K_1)$ and $G(L_2/K_2)$ are isomorphic if and only if the extensions $K_1 \subset L_1$ and $K_2 \subset L_2$ are isomorphic [@gismatullin2008, Introduction]. More precisely, for arbitrary extensions of transcendence degree at least $5$, every isomorphism of these geometries is induced by an isomorphism of the purely inseparable closures of $L$ and $L'$ [@gismatullin2008, Theorem 4.2].

**Algebraically closed constants.** For $k, k'$ algebraically closed and $K/k$, $K'/k'$ of transcendence degree at least $5$, every isomorphism of combinatorial geometries is induced by an isomorphism of purely inseparable closures. This is attributed to Evans–Hrushovski and Gismatullin [@bogomolovtschinkel2012, Theorem 7].

**Method.** The results stem from the group configuration theorem in geometric stability theory, and they play the role that the fundamental theorem of projective geometry plays in the pro-$\ell$ setting [@topaz2016, §1.3].

**Attribution.** The original papers of Evans and Hrushovski have not been opened for this entry.
