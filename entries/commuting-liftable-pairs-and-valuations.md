**Definition.** Suppose $\mu_{\ell^\infty} \subset K$. A pair $\sigma, \tau \in \Pi^a_K$ is commuting-liftable if lifts of them to $\Pi^c_K$ commute. Since $\Pi^c_K \to \Pi^a_K$ is central, this does not depend on the lifts. If $v$ is a valuation of $K$ whose residue characteristic is not $\ell$, with inertia and decomposition groups $T_v \subseteq Z_v$ in $\Pi^a_K$, then every $\sigma \in T_v$ and $\tau \in Z_v$ form such a pair [@topaz2017, §1.1].

**The converse.** Suppose $K$ also contains an algebraically closed subfield. The theory of Bogomolov and Tschinkel shows that a commuting-liftable pair arises through a valuation: some $\mathbb{Z}_\ell$-linear combination of $\sigma, \tau$ is a flag function, which corresponds under Kummer theory to an element of $T_v$ for some valuation $v$ [@topaz2017, §1.1].

A precise form: let $K$ contain a subfield $k$ with at least $11$ elements, and let $\gamma, \gamma' : K^\times \to R$ be nonproportional homomorphisms, with $R$ one of $\mathbb{Z}$, $\mathbb{Z}_\ell$, $\mathbb{Z}/\ell$. Suppose they are trivial on $k^\times$, and that on every projective line in $\mathbb{P}_k(K)$ the $R$-module spanned by $\gamma, \gamma', 1$ has rank at most $2$. Then some element in the span of $\gamma, \gamma'$ factors through the value group of a valuation of $K$ [@bogomolovtschinkel2012, Theorem 13].

**Function fields.** Let $K$ be a function field of dimension $n$ over an algebraically closed field. A non-procyclic liftable subgroup $\sigma \subseteq \Pi^a_K$ has rank at most $n$, and there is a valuation $\nu$ and a subgroup $\sigma' \subseteq \sigma$ with $\sigma'$ in the inertia of $\nu$, $\sigma$ in its decomposition group, and $\sigma/\sigma'$ topologically cyclic [@bogomolovtschinkel2012, Corollary 14].

**Finite level.** Topaz works with a modified notion. For $n$ a positive integer or $\infty$ with $\mu_{2\ell^n} \subset K$, let $G^{a,n}_K$ and $G^{c,n}_K$ be the maximal $\ell^n$-abelian and $\ell^n$-abelian-by-central Galois groups. A pair $\sigma, \tau \in G^{a,n}_K$ is a CL-pair if $[\sigma, \tau]$ lies in the group generated by $\sigma^\beta, \tau^\beta$, where $\sigma^\beta = 2 \cdot \sigma^\pi$ and $\sigma^\pi$ is the $\ell^n$-th power of a lift of $\sigma$. For $n = \infty$ this means $[\sigma, \tau] = 0$ [@topaz2017, §1.2].

Let $N \ge R(n)$ for an explicit function $R$ with $R(1) = 1$ and $R(\infty) = \infty$. Let $K$ have characteristic different from $\ell$ and contain $\mu_{2\ell^N}$. Then a subgroup $D$ of $G^{a,n}_K$ satisfies $D \le D^n_v$ with $D/(D \cap I^n_v)$ cyclic for some valuation $v$ if and only if $D$ is the image of a CL-group in $G^{a,N}_K$. Here $I^n_v \le D^n_v$ are [minimized](quasi-prime-divisor.md) inertia and decomposition groups, which are needed when the residue characteristic may be $\ell$. No algebraically closed subfield is required [@topaz2017, Theorem 1].

Under Kummer duality, the CL condition is the [vanishing of a determinant on Steinberg pairs](kummer-duality-for-commuting-lifts.md). Topaz proves the character-theoretic valuation results first, then uses this equivalence to obtain the Galois statements [@topaz2017, Theorem 12 and §8].

**Rigid elements.** A parallel theory uses rigid elements in the full pro-$\ell$ Galois group [@topaz2017, §1.1]. Topaz unifies these approaches [@topaz2017, §1, p. 3]. The [rigid-elements criterion and canonical valuations](rigid-subgroups-and-valuations.md) supply the subgroup-to-valuation step in his C-group argument [@topaz2017, Proposition 3.6, Lemmas 3.9–3.10 and Theorem 5].
