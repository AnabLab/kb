**Setting.** Fix a prime $\ell$, a number field $k$ and an algebraic closure $\bar k$. For a [hyperbolic curve](hyperbolic-curve.md) $D$ over a finite extension $L/k$ in $\bar k$, put
$$
\Delta_D^{(\ell)}=\pi_1(D_{\bar k})^{(\ell)},\qquad
\rho_{D/L}:G_L\longrightarrow\mathrm{Out}(\Delta_D^{(\ell)}),\qquad
\Omega_{D/L}=\bar k^{\ker\rho_{D/L}}.
$$
Here the superscript $(\ell)$ denotes the maximal pro-$\ell$ quotient, and $\rho_{D/L}$ is the natural outer Galois action [@hoshi2015, Definition 1.1, p. 652].

**Moderate points.** For $y\in D(L)$, an equivalent criterion for being *$\ell$-moderate* is
$$\Omega_{(D\setminus\{y\})/L}=\Omega_{D/L};$$
deleting $y$ does not enlarge the field detected by the outer action [@hoshi2015, Proposition 2.5, pp. 659–660]. For a closed point $x$ of a hyperbolic curve $C/k$, let $L_x\subset\bar k$ be the Galois closure of its residue field over $k$. The point $x$ is $\ell$-moderate if every $L_x$-rational point of $C_{L_x}$ above $x$ is $\ell$-moderate in this sense [@hoshi2015, Definitions 2.3–2.4, p. 659]. Define $M_{C,\ell}$ to be the compositum in $\bar k$ of all such $L_x$ for $\ell$-moderate closed points $x$; this is Hoshi's field $k_C^{\mathrm{mdr}\text{-}\ell}$ [@hoshi2015, Remark 2.6.2, p. 662].

**Question (Hoshi).** Does
$$M_{C,\ell}=\Omega_{C/k}$$
hold for every hyperbolic curve $C$ over a number field and every prime $\ell$? Hoshi poses this in [@hoshi2015, Remark 2.6.2, p. 662] and leaves the general equality open [@hoshi2015, Introduction, p. 649]. The source checked here does not resolve the general question.

**Known.** Every $\ell$-moderate point is defined over $\Omega_{C/k}$, so $M_{C,\ell}\subseteq\Omega_{C/k}$ always [@hoshi2015, Theorem 3.1, p. 664]. Equality holds in particular for:

- $C=\mathbb P^1_k\setminus\{0,1,\infty\}$, for every $\ell$ [@hoshi2015, Corollary 3.3, pp. 666–667].
- The affine Fermat curve $s^{\ell^n}+t^{\ell^n}+1=0$ for odd $\ell$ and $n\ge1$ [@hoshi2015, Example 3.4(i), pp. 667–668].
- The projective Fermat curve $s^{\ell^n}+t^{\ell^n}+u^{\ell^n}=0$ for odd $\ell$ and $n\ge1$, excluding $(\ell,n)=(3,1)$ [@hoshi2015, Example 3.4(ii), p. 668].
- For $\ell=3$, the full-level modular curve $Y(3^n)$ when $n\ge1$ and $\mu_{3^n}\subset k$, and its smooth compactification $X(3^n)$ when $n\ge2$ under the same root-of-unity hypothesis [@hoshi2015, Example 3.4(iv)–(v), pp. 669–670].

Hoshi also records weaker targets: finiteness of $[\Omega_{C/k}:M_{C,\ell}]$ implies infinitely many $\ell$-moderate closed points, hence existence of one [@hoshi2015, Remark 3.1.1(i), pp. 664–665].

**Relation to the tripod question.** For the tripod, generation by moderate points is already proved for every prime. The [Anderson–Ihara question](anderson-ihara-question.md) instead asks whether, over $\mathbb Q$, the same kernel field is the maximal pro-$\ell$ extension of $\mathbb Q(\mu_\ell)$ unramified outside $\ell$; Hoshi distinguishes these geometric and arithmetic descriptions [@hoshi2015, Remark 1.8.1, p. 657; Remark 2.6.2, p. 662; Corollary 3.3, pp. 666–667].
