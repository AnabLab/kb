Let $q=p^s$ with $s\ge1$, let $F$ contain a primitive $q$th root of unity, and put $G=G_F$. All cohomology below is continuous, with trivial coefficients $\mathbb Z/q$.

**The quotient.** Set
$$
G^{(2)}=G^q[G,G],\qquad
K_q(G)=G^{\delta q}[G^{(2)},G],\qquad
Q_q(G)=G/K_q(G),\qquad
\delta=\begin{cases}1&p>2,\\2&p=2.\end{cases}
$$
Products and generated subgroups are closed. This is Efrat–Mináč's $G_{[3]}$. The third descending $q$-central term $(G^{(2)})^q[G^{(2)},G]$ is contained in $K_q(G)$; equality holds when $q=2$. When $q=p$, $K_p(G)$ is the third $p$-Zassenhaus term [@efratminac2017functors, §1, pp. 1–2, and §2, equation (2.1) and Remark 2.1].

**Minimality by inflation.** Write $H^*(P)_{\mathrm{dec}}$ for the subring generated by $H^1(P)$. For every closed normal $N\trianglelefteq G$,
$$
\inf:H^*(G/N)_{\mathrm{dec}}\longrightarrow H^*(G)
\quad\text{is an isomorphism}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad N\subseteq K_q(G).
$$
Thus $Q_q(G)$ is the smallest quotient with this inflation property [@efratminac2017functors, Theorem A]. Conversely, $H^1(G)$, $H^2(G)$ and their cup product determine $Q_q(G)$, without a separate Bockstein map [@efratminac2017functors, Theorem B and Corollary 7.3(1)].

**Detecting isomorphisms of maps.** If $F_1,F_2$ contain primitive $q$th roots of unity and $\pi:G_{F_1}(p)\to G_{F_2}(p)$ is a continuous homomorphism of maximal pro-$p$ Galois groups, then $\pi$ is an isomorphism exactly when its induced map on $Q_q$ is an isomorphism, equivalently when $\pi^*$ on mod-$q$ cohomology is an isomorphism [@efratminac2017functors, Theorem C]. This statement starts with the homomorphism $\pi$; it does not assert that every abstract isomorphism of the small quotients lifts.

**Finite quotients at $q=p$.** Here $K_p(G)$ is the intersection of open normal subgroups $N$ with the following quotients:

- For odd $p$, $G/N\cong\mathbb Z/p$ or $H_{p^3}$, where $H_{p^3}=U_3(\mathbb F_p)$ is the Heisenberg group of order $p^3$ and exponent $p$ [@efratminac2017functors, Theorem D].
- For $p=2$, $G/N\cong\mathbb Z/2$, $\mathbb Z/4$ or the dihedral group of order $8$ [@efratminac2017functors, Example 9.5(1)].

Equivalently, $K_p(G)=\bigcap_\rho\ker\rho$ over continuous $\rho:G\to U_3(\mathbb F_p)$, with $U_3$ the upper triangular unipotent matrices [@efratminac2017functors, §1, p. 4]. For $q=p$, $Q_q(G)$ is also the quotient used to [detect compatible valuations](small-galois-quotients-detect-valuations.md) [@efratminac2017functors, §1, p. 3].
