**Question.** The [theorem on combinatorial geometries](combinatorial-geometry-reconstruction.md) requires transcendence degree at least $5$. Does it hold in transcendence degree $3$ or $4$?

**Known.** The assumption is required in a technical step that determines which group configurations arise from the multiplicative or additive group. According to Topaz, the first page of Evans–Hrushovski's paper says similar results are expected for transcendence degree at least $3$, although this is still open [@topaz2016, §1.3].

**Status.** The statement that this is open is from a paper whose arXiv version is dated 2015. A literature search in November 2026 found no answer, but that search was not exhaustive. Evans–Hrushovski's paper itself has not been opened for this entry.
