**Setup.** Fix a prime $\ell$, an integer $n\ge1$, and $m=\ell^n$. Let $K$ have characteristic different from $\ell$ with $\mu_{2m}\subset K$, and choose a primitive $m$-th root $\omega$. For the absolute Galois group $G$ put
$$
G^{(2)}=\overline{[G,G]G^m},\qquad
G^{(3)}=\overline{[G,G^{(2)}](G^{(2)})^m},\qquad
A=G/G^{(2)},\quad C=G/G^{(3)}.
$$
Kummer duality identifies $A$ with $\operatorname{Hom}(K^\times,\mathbb Z/m)$. For $\sigma,\tau\in A$, write $f,g$ for their characters. In the central kernel of $C\to A$, write $\sigma^\pi$ for the $m$-th power of a lift. The CL condition is $[\sigma,\tau]\in\langle2\sigma^\pi,2\tau^\pi\rangle$ [@topaz2017abc, §1.1 and §5.1].

**Criterion.** The pair is CL if and only if
$$
f(x)g(1-x)-f(1-x)g(x)=0
\quad\text{for every }x\in K\setminus\{0,1\}
$$
[@topaz2017, Definition 2.1 and Theorem 12]. Equivalently, for $T=\ker f\cap\ker g$, the map
$$
f\wedge g:\bigwedge^2(K^\times/T)\longrightarrow\mathbb Z/m,
\qquad a\wedge b\longmapsto f(a)g(b)-f(b)g(a)
$$
factors through $K^M_2(K)/T$. Here this quotient means the degree-two part of the Milnor K-ring modulo the ideal generated by $T$ in degree one [@topaz2017, §6 and proof of Proposition 6.1].

**Actual commuting lifts.** At finite level the CL condition allows a nonzero commutator. Topaz's explicit embedding of $G^{(2)}/G^{(3)}$ into functions on $K\setminus\{0,1\}$ sends $[\sigma,\tau]$ to
$$
x\longmapsto\big(f(x)g(1-x)-f(1-x)g(x),\,
f(x)g(\omega)-f(\omega)g(x)\big).
$$
Thus lifts commute exactly when both coordinates vanish [@topaz2017abc, §1.1 and Theorem 1].

**Pro-$\ell$ version.** If $\mu_{\ell^\infty}\subset K$, use $\mathbb Z_\ell$-valued characters and the quotients $\Pi_K^a,\Pi_K^c$ from [abelian-by-central Galois groups](abelian-by-central-galois-group.md). Then the displayed Steinberg determinant criterion is equivalent to commuting lifts in $\Pi_K^c$ [@topaz2017, Theorem 12, the $n=\infty$ case]. This is the multiplicative-character form of the [local theory recovering valuations](commuting-liftable-pairs-and-valuations.md).
