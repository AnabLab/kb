**Statement.** Let $K$ and $L$ be function fields over algebraic closures $k, l$ of finite fields, of characteristic different from $\ell$, with $K$ of transcendence degree at least $2$ over $k$. Let $\Sigma_K$ be the set of non-procyclic subgroups of $\Pi^a_K$ that lift to abelian subgroups of $\Pi^c_K$. Suppose $\Psi : \Pi^a_K \to \Pi^a_L$ is an isomorphism inducing a bijection $\Sigma_K \to \Sigma_L$. Then $k = l$, and there is a constant $\epsilon \in \mathbb{Z}_\ell^\times$ such that $\epsilon^{-1} \cdot \Psi$ is induced by a unique isomorphism of perfect closures $L^i \to K^i$ [@bogomolovtschinkel2012, Theorem 2].

**Status of the conjecture in this case.** The conjecture is settled here by Bogomolov–Tschinkel and separately by Pop [@topaz2016, §1]. See also [@pop2018, §1]. Over these constants, quasi-divisorial valuations are exactly the divisorial ones [@topaz2016, §1.1], which is one reason the local and global theories fit together.

**Attribution.** The statements are taken from the sources above. The original papers of Bogomolov–Tschinkel (Geom. Funct. Anal. 2008, Moscow Math. J. 2011) and Pop (Invent. Math. 2012) have not been opened for this entry.
